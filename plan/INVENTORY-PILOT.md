## The sourcing finding, and it is a real constraint

The obtainability probe ran before authoring, and the result is structural rather than incidental.

| Source kind | Assigned to | Obtainable here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `primary`, `systematic_review` | empirical concepts | **Yes** — resolves through PMC and open-access routes; verified on a live DOI |
| `textbook` | **derivable** concepts | **No** — no route to book text from this environment |
| `instrument`, `guideline` | **institutional** concepts | **Largely no** — Indian statutes, FSSAI notifications, GST Council decisions and court orders sit on domains outside the network allowlist |

The bite: S01 is 13 of 17 concepts derivable and 4 empirical, and S48 is 20 of 20 institutional. **Both pilots are dominated by exactly the two source kinds that cannot be obtained from here, while the one kind that can be obtained is barely used by either.** That is not an argument for relaxing the rule — `verified.opened` must never be set for a source nobody opened, and a plausible-looking citation is worse than an absent one. It is an argument that citation verification is a step requiring library and government-portal access, and it belongs to whoever has them.

**What this changes.** Chapters can be drafted to `status: drafted` with candidate references carrying `opened: false` and an honest note, and the build already warns on every one of them and refuses to let them reach `verified`. What cannot happen from inside this environment is the transition to `verified`, and therefore to `released`. Three ways forward, in order of preference:

1. **Request the specific domains** — India Code, FSSAI, the Supreme Court and PIB are a small, nameable set that would unlock most of S48's twenty citations.
2. **Verify against what is reachable** where a primary or consensus source legitimately defines the concept, rather than reaching for a textbook by reflex. Several S01 concepts are better anchored in Hall's dynamic energy balance papers than in a textbook, and those resolve.
3. **Hand the textbook citations to a reader with library access** as a discrete, listed task — thirteen for S01, one per derivable concept — rather than leaving them as a diffuse obligation.
