"3.6 Food energy  is expressed in kilojoules (kJ) 
calculated based on the Atwater system for determining 
energy values ( Table 4 ). The data represent 
physiologically available energy, which is the energy 
value remaining after digestive and urinary losses are 
deducted from gross energy. Food energy in kilojoules 
(kJ) can be converted using the formula 1 kcal = 4.18 
kJ ."
