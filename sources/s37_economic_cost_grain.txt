"14. Pooled Cost: Pooled cost of grains refers to Purchase of Food grains at MSP.  
 
 14. पूल लागि: अनाज की पूल लार्त से तात्पयव एमएसपी पर खाद्यान्न की खरीद से है। 
15. Procurement Incidental: The Procurement Incidentals of food grains refer s costs incurred in 
the process of procuring food grains, over and above the Minimum Support Price (MSP). These 
expenses include mandi labour charges,statutory levies,commission to societies/arthatia,cost of 
gunny bags, transportation & handling charges, storage expenses, interest cost, administrative 
cost and other related expenditures. 
15. खिीद संबंिी आकतस्मक व्यय:  खाद्यान्नों की खरीद संबंिी आकक्षस्मक व्यय का अथव, न्यूनतम समथवन मूल्य 
(एमएसपी) के  अक्षतररक्त, खाद्यान्नों की खरीद प्रक्षिया में होने र्ाली लार्त है। इन व्ययों में मंडी श्रम शुल्क, संर्ैिाक्षनक 
उर्ाही, सक्षमक्षतयों/आढ़क्षतयों को क्षदया जाने र्ाला कमीशन, बोररयों की लार्त, पररर्हन एर्ं हैंडक्षलंर् शुल्क, भंडारण व्यय, 
सयाज लार्त, प्रशासक्षनक लार्त और अन्य संबंक्षित व्यय शाक्षमल हैं। 
16. Acquisition Cost: The Acquisition cost involves the cost of procuring food grains , packaging 
it and delivering it to FCI/ State storage point. These expenses include Minimum Support Price 
(MSP), mandi labour charges, statutory levies, commission to societies/ arhatia, cost of gunny 
bags, transportation & handling charges, storage expenses , interest cost, administrative cost 
and other related expenses. In case of paddy procurement , it also includes the cost of driage 
and milling charges. 
16.   अतिग्रहण लागि:  अक्षिग्रहण लार्त में खाद्यान्नों की खरीद, उनकी पैके क्षजंर् और उन्हें एफसीआई/राज्य भंडारण कें द्र 
तक पह ुँचाने की लार्त शाक्षमल है। इन खचों में न्यूनतम समथवन मूल्य (एमएसपी), मंडी श्रम शुल्क, संर्ैिाक्षनक उर्ाही, 
सक्षमक्षतयों/आढ़क्षतयों को क्षदया जाने र्ाला कमीशन, बोररयों की लार्त, पररर्हन एर्ं हैंडक्षलंर् शुल्क, भंडारण व्यय, सयाज 
लार्त, प्रशासक्षनक लार्त और अन्य संबंक्षित व्यय शाक्षमल हैं। िान खरीद के  मामले में, इसमें सुखाने (ड्राईएज) की लार्त 
और क्षमक्षलंर् शुल्क भी शाक्षमल है। 
17. Distribution Cost: The distribution cost involves the cost of distribution from storage point to 
the designated depots. These expenses include storage charges, handling & transportation 
charges, interest charges, transit and storage losses and administrative cost etc. 
17.  तवििण लागि: क्षर्तरण लार्त में भंडारण कें द्र से नाक्षमत क्षडपो तक क्षर्तरण की लार्त शाक्षमल होती है। इन व्यय में 
भंडारण शुल्क, हैंडक्षलंर् और पररर्हन शुल्क, सयाज शुल्क, पारर्मन और भंडारण हाक्षनयाुँ और प्रशासक्षनक लार्त आक्षद 
शाक्षमल हैं। 
18.  Economic Cost: The Economic Cost for a Decentralized Procurement (DCP) State is the total 
of all incidentals incurred during both the Acquisition and Distribution stages. It is computed 
as Economic Cost = Acquisition Cost + Distribution Cost.

18.  आतथषक लागि: क्षकसी क्षर्के न्द्रीकृ त खरीद (डीसीपी) राज्य की आक्षथवक लार्त, अक्षिग्रहण और क्षर्तरण दोनों चरणों के  
दौरान होने र्ाले सभी आकक्षस्मक व्यय का योर् है। इसकी र्णना आक्षथवक लार्त = अक्षिग्रहण लार्त + क्षर्तरण लार्त

19. Central Issue Price (CIP):  The price at which the central government issues food grains to 
state governments for distribution through the PDS.  Foodgrains are being provided to the 
targeted beneficiaries at highly subsidized rates for various Central Schemes under National 
Food Security Act (NFSA) 2013 and Other Welfare Schemes (OWS)."

"The Central Government, in order to remove the financial burden of the poor beneficiaries and to ensure nationwide uniformity and effective implementation of the Act, had decided to provide foodgrains free of cost to NFSA beneficiaries i.e. AAY households and PHH beneficiaries,for a period of one year beginning from 1st January 2023 under the Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY). The period of distribution for free of cost food grains under the PMGKAY has been extended for five years w.e.f. 1st January, 2024."

"7.         Support to Farmers

Procurement Operation: The main objectives of food management are procurement of foodgrains from farmers at remunerative prices, distribution of foodgrains to consumers, particularly the vulnerable sections of society at affordable prices and maintenance of buffer stock for food security and price stability. The Central Government extends price support to paddy, coarse grains and wheat through the FCI and State Agencies. All the foodgrains (wheat and paddy) conforming to the prescribed specifications offered for sale at specified centres are bought by the public procurement agencies at the Minimum Support Price (MSP) inclusive of bonus announced, if any. The farmers have the option to sell their produce to FCI/State Agencies at MSP or in the open market as is advantageous to them.

During Rabi Marketing Season 2025-26, a quantity of 300.35 LMT of wheat was procured through which 2513143 number of farmers were benefited.

During Kharif Marketing Season 2024-25, a quantity of 832.17 LMT of paddy has been procured through which 11858507 number of farmers were benefited. During ongoing KMS 2025-26, 243.48 LMT of paddy has been procured and 2122273 number of farmers have benefited till 17.11.2025."

Entities



Wheat Stock Limit




Traders / Wholesalers



2000 MT




Retailers



8 MT for each Retail outlet.




Big Chain Retailers



Upto 8 MT for each retail outlet subject to maximum quantity of (8 multiplied by total no. of outlets) MT. This will be the maximum stock that can be held at all their retail outlets and depots put together




Processors



60% of Monthly Installed Capacity (MIC) multiplied by remaining months of FY 2025-26."
