[NOTE] This file's own gloss, NOT source text and NOT quotable: the Food Corporation of India was set up
[NOTE] under the Food Corporations Act 1964 to fulfil the following objectives of food policy: effective

[NOTE] operational and buffer stocks of foodgrains to ensure national food security. Since its inception
[NOTE] FCI has played a significant role in India's success in transforming crisis-management-oriented food
[NOTE] security into a stable security system.
