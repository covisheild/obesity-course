WHAT THIS FILE HOLDS: EXCERPTS. The cover lines; the paragraph giving the fieldwork dates and sample
(636,699 households, 724,115 women, 101,839 men); the column headers of the key-indicators table;
indicators 81-98 (child stunting, wasting, severe wasting, underweight, overweight; adult BMI below

HOW TO READ THE TABLE (this file's reading of the column header, not the source's words): the four
numbers in each row are NFHS-5 (2019-21) Urban, Rural, Total, then NFHS-4 (2015-16) Total, in per
cent. So child stunting is 35.5% in NFHS-5 against 38.4% in NFHS-4; women overweight or obese 24.0%
against 20.6%; men 22.9% against 18.9%. The adult rows cover age 15-49 only; the fact sheet has no
"obese" row separate from "overweight or obese".
