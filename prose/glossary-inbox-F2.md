# Glossary inbox · F2

New terms taught in `B0-R0-C40` (F2, reading a figure). Held here rather than written into
`GLOSSARY.md` because the Part D and E chats were adding rows at the same time, and `PARALLEL.md`
routes concurrent additions through an inbox for the human to merge.

| Term | Plain words it gets at first use | First taught in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bin | a range of values grouped together into one bar | B0-R0-C40 |
| infographic | a chart decorated with pictures | B0-R0-C40 |
| mark | anything drawn that stands for one number: a bar, a point, a slice, a picture | B0-R0-C40 |
| scale factor | the number every length of a picture is multiplied by | B0-R0-C40 |
| square law | multiply every length of a flat picture by a factor and its area is multiplied by that factor squared | B0-R0-C40 |
| whisker | a short line through a point, showing a spread or an uncertainty, which the caption should name | B0-R0-C40 |
| window | the stretch of time a chart over time shows | B0-R0-C40 |
