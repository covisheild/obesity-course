   - **Claims:**

   - **What the source says:** confirmed, and worse than the hole said.

     - FAO calls these "energy requirements" ("would have a mean energy requirement of 1.75 × 7.10",
       block 7), the second kind in this section's own scheme.
   - **Smallest fix:** make the first kind "a calculation of what a typical group would spend: an
     activity multiplier times a resting rate (FAO/WHO/UNU's worked examples)". Cut "In three groups
     it measured" and "A measured group". Doubly labelled water stays as the method that would
     measure it. If the section must show a real measured group mean, it needs a new source (a

   - **Tag:** error. **Recurring** (SELFCHECK 1: a quote made to stand behind a claim it does not
     make).

2. **`illustration.body`: FAO against ICMR "for the same activity band"** (from compression hole 25)
   - **Claim:** "within about three hundred kilocalories of the Indian committee's 1,660 for the same
     activity band."
   - **What the source says:** 1,975 − 1,660 = 315, so the arithmetic holds. But the held ICMR note
     explains the gap, and the section instead calls the bands "the same".
     - Both are 55 kg women: FAO's example "mean weight of 55 kg"; ICMR Table 1b, women 55.0.
     - ICMR cut the sedentary PAL "from 1.53 that was used in the previous recommendation (RDA-2010)
       to 1.40 for sedentary population".
     - ICMR also cut BMR 5% plus "another 5 %" below the FAO/WHO/UNU equations.
     - ICMR's figure is itself "calculated based on a multiplication of basal metabolic rate (BMR)
       to physical activity level (PAL): TEE = BMR X PAL".

     So the two numbers are the same calculation with different inputs. That is the best lesson in
     the section on "say which kind", and it is missing.
   - **Smallest fix:** replace the sentence with: "Both figures are for 55 kg women. ICMR's is lower
     because the committee lowered the sedentary activity multiplier from FAO's 1.53 to 1.40 and cut
     the resting rate for Indians by a further 5%. Both are BMR times PAL, not measurements."
   - **Tag:** error. **Recurring** (SELFCHECK 4).
