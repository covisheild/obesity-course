# Audit defects · S01-R1-C09 · Intake and expenditure are outputs of a regulated system

Task 3 audit, 23 September 2026. Sources opened: `sources/rosenbaum_leibel_2010.txt`,
`sources/polidori_2016.txt`, `sources/hall_guo_2017.txt` (the files INDEX.yml maps the citekeys
to). Polidori's full text was also read through PubMed Central (PMC5108589, doi
10.1002/oby.21653) to settle items the held excerpts do not cover; those items say so, and
the passages they depend on are **not in the held file**, so they cannot carry a quote until the
source pack is extended.

**Quotes.** All four definition quotes and all four `illustration.numbers` quotes are verbatim in
the held files and each states the number it is cited for (20%-25%, 10–15%, ~300–400,
~100 kcal/day per kg, 3-fold). No quote field needed changing. Items 1, 5, 6 and 8 below are
cases where a quote is genuine but the sentence it backs says something else.

**Arithmetic (Python).** 8 × 100 = 800. 800 / 400 = 2.0 and 800 / 300 = 2.67. An 8 kg loss is
10% only at 8 / 0.10 = 80 kg. Polidori's own comparison: 100 / 30 = 3.33 ("more than 3-fold").
At 8 kg on that basis, 8 × 30 = 240 and 800 / 240 = 3.33. 300 to 400 kcal as 10% to 15% of
expenditure puts expenditure at 2,000 to 4,000 kcal a day, so the figures agree with each other.
Polidori's steady-state rise in intake of ~350 kcal a day at a fitted 95 kcal a day per kg means
the trial reached about 350 / 95 = 3.7 kg lost. No practice set (see item 17).

**"Recurring"** means the kind is on `check/SELFCHECK.md`, whose items are kinds found more than
once in the Book 0 audits (`books/B0/defects/`).

1. **error**, **recurring** (SELFCHECK 1, 7) · `definition.text`, illustration last paragraph,
   figure caption (from compression hole 56). The definition says the appetite pull "is more than
   three times the size of the matching fall in expenditure". The illustration then sets 800 kcal
   (8 kg × 100) beside 300 to 400 kcal. That ratio is 2 to 2.7, and the two numbers match only
   for a person of 80 kg. **What the source says:** Polidori's "3-fold" compares like with like,
   per kilogram lost: "energy intake increased by ~100 kcal/day per kg of weight lost—an effect
   substantially greater than the ~30 kcal/kg/day changes in energy expenditure observed with 10
   to 20% weight loss in subjects with obesity" (held file, Results). 100 / 30 = 3.3. The ~30 is
   the whole change in expenditure per kilogram, not Rosenbaum and Leibel's adaptive remainder.
   So the definition's claim is sourced, and the illustration's comparison is the wrong one.
   **Smallest fix:** definition: "Per kilogram lost, eating rises by about 100 kcal a day, more
   than three times the fall of about 30 kcal a day in expenditure." Add a Polidori reference
   quoting the Results sentence above. Illustration: "The matching fall in expenditure, at about
   30 kcal a day per kilogram, is about 240 kcal a day." Remove the 300-to-400 comparison from
   that paragraph, because the 300 to 400 kcal is a different quantity (item 2).
   Fixer: definition now compares per kilogram (about 100 vs about 30 kcal a day), with a Polidori Results reference quoting the ~30 kcal/kg/day sentence; illustration's 800-vs-300-to-400 comparison removed and replaced by a per-kilogram one (item 9); caption rewritten.
