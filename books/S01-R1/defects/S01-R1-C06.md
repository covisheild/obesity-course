1. **error** · `illustration.body` and retrieval item 2: the missing premise is stated too
   strongly.
   - Claim: the hidden premise is "and expenditure does not change when intake does". Retrieval
     item 2 gives it as "That expenditure does not change when intake changes."
   - The conclusion ("their energy stores will fall") needs only that expenditure falls by less
     than intake fell. Section 4 (`must_know[2]`) tells the reader to expect expenditure to fall as
     weight falls. So the premise as written is one the reader already knows to be false. That
     invites the wrong lesson: that eating less cannot lower stores. The illustration's own
     counter-case ("fell by the same amount") shows where the line actually sits.
   - Fix: "and expenditure does not fall by as much as intake did." In retrieval item 2: "That
     expenditure does not fall by as much as intake is cut."
   Fixer: applied both wordings (illustration's missing premise; retrieval item 2 answer).
