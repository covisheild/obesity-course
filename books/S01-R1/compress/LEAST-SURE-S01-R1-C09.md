# S01-R1-C09 · least-sure cut (pass 1)

Every sentence saying the trial's weight loss was *not* a diet ("nobody told them to eat less"; "lost weight without dieting at all"; "without the person knowing a deficit had been created") is gone. Only the breaks-when field still says "not from dieting". That fact is what makes the 100 kcal/kg rise read as biology rather than willpower, which is exactly what Exercise 1 asks the reader to argue.
