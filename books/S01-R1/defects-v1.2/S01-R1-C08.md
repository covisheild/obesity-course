# S01-R1-C08 · external reader feedback, 24 Sep 2026 (triaged by the conductor)

1. **error** — practice 8 (140 kg patient, 12 kg in 21 days) answer. It scales Section 3's 36 kcal
   per kg a day (a 55 kg woman) linearly to 140 kg, giving a 5,040 kcal/day ceiling, and concludes
   "the check cannot decide". Expenditure does not scale in proportion to body mass: fat tissue
   adds far less to expenditure than fat-free mass (the section itself and `S01-R1-C01` say so).
   Smallest fix: bound expenditure with a held, weight-based equation instead. FAO/WHO/UNU 2004
   Table 5.2 (Schofield BMR equations; now in `sources/fao_who_unu_2004.txt`) gives, at 140 kg,
   about 1,983 (women 30–60) to 2,800 (men 18–30) kcal/day BMR. Take the most generous band and
   the book's sedentary PAL 1.53 (already used in Section 3), intake zero: 2,800.2 × 1.53 × 21 ÷
   7,716.18 ≈ 11.66 kg < 12 (verify each number in Python from the held coefficients; quote the
   table rows). Every other band gives less, and real post-surgery intake is above zero. So 12 kg
   of pure fat in 21 days is ruled out, with a thin margin in the most generous case. Say also that
   Schofield's equations were fitted on lighter people, so using them at 140 kg is itself an
   extrapolation, and name that as what the reader must check. Keep "What this does not establish"
   (glycogen, water, lean tissue). Do NOT use the reviewer's unsourced numbers (Mifflin-St Jeor,
   2,500–2,800 kcal TDEE, organ rates).
