# S01-R1-C02 · external reader feedback, 24 Sep 2026 (triaged by the conductor)

1. **error** — definition/plain text: "FAO's factors are corrected for what is lost in digestion,
   absorption and urine. They describe fat eaten." Applied to fat this is wrong: FAO FNP 77 says
   the urinary loss is "compounds derived from incomplete catabolism of protein" (held,
   `sources/fao_food_energy.txt`). Fix: the factors are corrected for what is lost in the faeces,
   and for protein also in the urine; quote FAO.
2. **error** — "32.19 … close to the famous rule's 32.2, but partly by luck" (lines ~155, 248, 310,
   496, 630, 660 and wherever else). Hall 2008's own words: "3500 kcal … per pound … or
   equivalently 32.2 MJ per kg" (held): 32.2 is the rule restated in SI units, not a separate
   estimate. Exact conversion: 3,500 ÷ 0.45359237 = 7,716.18 kcal/kg = 32.29 MJ/kg; 7,700 kcal/kg
   gives 32.2. Fix: say 32.2 is the 3,500 kcal/pound rule in megajoules (Hall writes "equivalently");
   the rule's origin is a calculation assuming 87% fat (Hall, held). So 0.87 × 37 = 32.19 landing
   near it does not confirm or reconstruct the rule: other fat values give other answers (keep the
   37.4 / 39.5 point). Drop the word "luck". Do not add the reviewer's account of Wishnofsky's
   numbers (not held).
