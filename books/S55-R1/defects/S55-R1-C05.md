1. **illustration[0] test one ("You have no figure ... so you cannot say what the share will be. It passes"), and
   exercise 1 item 3 answer ("In doubt: yes, because you cannot guess the number")**. The section defines the test
   as "write down two different answers you might get, with a reason to expect each". Retrieval 3 says "If you can
   find a reason for only one, the answer is not in doubt". Not knowing the figure is not a reason for two answers.
   In both places the worked case passes a candidate by a rule the section itself rejects, and this is the
   section's first worked use of test one. Fix: give two reasons. Illustration: two made-up reasons, one for a low
   share and one for a higher one, each stated as made up (no claim about the real district). Ex 1 item 3: "a reason for few: patients are booked by condition, not
   by person; a reason for many: the clinic may book everyone it sees for both checks" (reasons about this made-up clinic only, not claims about the world). **error**

2. **retrieval_items[1] "Which three of Ioannidis's features ... match those tests? Problem base ..., context
   placement ... and information gain ..."** The section never makes this mapping, and it does not hold. Ioannidis
   defines information gain as "Is the proposed study large and long enough to be sufficiently informative?" That
   is a size check, and the illustration itself uses it as a separate check after the three tests. It is not "is the
   answer in doubt". Problem base ("a health problem big/important enough to fix") is not "would the answer change a
   decision" either. Fix: ask "Which two of Ioannidis's features did the worked example use, and for what?" and
   answer "context placement (find what is known before planning) and information gain (enough people for the
   answer to be acted on)". **error**
