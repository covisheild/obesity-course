# Found by the compression pass · Part B

Run 22 September 2026. Step 5b's cold reader worked every exercise and every numbered problem
in the six cut sections and reported where it was stranded. These are the gaps that **restoring
text could not close**, because the original does not fill them either. They go back through
the audit.

## Real defects in the material

1. **B6 · `illustration.body` — the index is never named.** The noun "the index" arrives already
   definite and is used eleven times without the section ever saying what it is, what it is for,
   or who uses it. The reader computed it correctly eleven times and never learned what they had
   computed. This is present in the original, not created by the cut.

2. **B6 · `illustration.body` — a division is asserted, not shown.** The worked example prints
   `168 times 168 = 28,224` and then gives 0.00248 without the division line, in a section whose
   own standard is that worked calculations show every line. The instruction to divide was
   restored from the original; the line itself was never there.

3. **B6 · practice level 5 requires rearranging to find a missing mass**, and no section in Part
   B ever rearranges an equation. The move is assumed rather than taught.

4. **B3 · `definition.text` states a rule about logarithms** — that a quantity used as an
   exponent carries no unit — and repeats it twice more. No worked example uses a logarithm and
   none of the fourteen problems exercises it. Either the rule needs the demonstration, or it
   belongs in A7 where logarithms are taught.

5. **B5 · the reason given for withholding cholesterol's molar mass is the course's own
   data-capture history.** The withholding itself worked: the cold reader reached for a divisor
   at two problems, found none, checked whether glucose's could be borrowed, and stopped, which
   is exactly the intended experience. But the stated reason — that the figure was not captured
   when the page was read — is the course narrating its own accident rather than teaching. The
   reader reported that it made them "hold every other figure in the set slightly more loosely".
   The pedagogical reason is the true one and is better: you cannot convert without the molar
   mass of the particular substance, and looking it up is the move.

## A finding about the method, not the material

6. **The cold read was given Part B alone, and roughly half its gap report is an artifact of
   that.** The reader flagged "crore" and "lakh" as undefined, the Indian digit grouping as
   unexplained, "the section on powers" as a dangling cross-reference, and the
   how-many-digits-to-write rule as missing. All four are taught in Part A: A1 for the grouping
   and the lakh and crore names, A6 for powers, A3 for significant figures.

   The skill says to batch the cold read once per Part. That was written when Part A was the
   only Part and nothing preceded it. **Book 0's Parts build on each other, so from Part B
   onward the cold reader must be given every earlier Part as well** — otherwise every legitimate
   backward dependency reads as a hole, and the real gaps have to be dug out of the false ones.
   The scratch directory stays the isolation mechanism; it just has to hold the sections the
   reader would genuinely have read by that point.
