# S47-R1 step 5c, batch r2: holes for the fixer (C07 to C12)

Each hole: section and field, the sentence as it stands in the final text (or the exercise), what is
wrong or missing, what would close it. Gap ids refer to `COLD-READ-GAPS.md`. "Residual" marks a gap
that was restored in part, with what the original could not supply listed here.

**H-r2-1 (C07-2).** C07 `must_know[3].point`: "Section 93 keeps a made regulation before Parliament
for {{n:laying_days}} days of sitting." "Days of sitting" is never explained. Close: one sentence
saying it counts only days a House actually sits, so the period can run across sessions.

**H-r2-2 (C07-3).** C07 `must_know[2].point`: "Count the objection period from the day the notice
names." The counting convention (start day included? end day?) is not taught, and C11 P6 asks for
dates. Close: state the rule from a held source (General Clauses Act section 9 excludes the first
day and includes the last), or say the reader should count conservatively.

**H-r2-3 (C07-5, residual).** C07 `definition.text`: the order of previous publication and previous
approval is implied only by "It, and any authority whose sanction ... must consider any objection".
Sections 92(1) and 94(1) list the two in opposite orders. Close: one sentence saying the order of the
words is not the order of the steps, and that the approving body acts after objections close.

**H-r2-4 (C07-6), possible error.** C07 `must_know[0].point`: "Section 93 lays each rule and
regulation before Parliament after it is made." The original's Definition says "Every rule and
regulation made under that Act". Section 93 covers Central rules and Food Authority regulations;
State rules under section 94 are laid before the State Legislature (section 94(3), to be checked
against the held Act). Ex2 cannot be completed. Close: verify section 94(3) and say where State rules
are laid.

**H-r2-5 (C07-7).** C07 `must_know[5].point`: "The 2014 consultation policy binds officials as a
decision of the Committee of Secretaries, not as a law." How a non-statutory decision binds is not
explained, and C06 said only "not a statute". Close: say it binds as an internal executive
instruction, enforceable inside government but not in court, or soften "binds".

**H-r2-6 (C08-2).** C08 `definition.text`: "Mission Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0 is one."
"Anganwadi" is undefined; must_know[4] cites NFSA sections 4, 5 and 6 without saying what they give.
Close: gloss Anganwadi (village childcare and nutrition centre) and the entitlements in a clause each.

**H-r2-7 (C08-5).** C08 `must_know[5].point`: "2030-31 for the oilseeds mission" uses the mission
before it is introduced (Ex2 introduces it). The original's definition sentence fails the
mean-sentence gate if restored. Close: name the mission in must_know[5] itself, or have the build
order Ex2 first.

**H-r2-8 (C08-6).** C08 figure "60 general, 25 salary, 50 supplementary nutrition": the components are
not explained in prose, and the 25% salary share appears nowhere in the text. Close: one sentence per
component from the 2022 guidelines, or drop the components the prose does not explain.

**H-r2-9 (C08-7).** C08 Ex2 and definition: "Rs 10,103 crore". "Crore" (and A4's "lakh") never defined.
Close: a gloss on first use (1 crore = 10 million) or a book-level glossary entry.

**H-r2-10 (C08-8, C11-7).** C08 `simplified_explanation` and C11 `definition.text` step 3: for a scheme,
the "numbered provision" Y is never named (Article 73/162 executive power? Article 282? a Cabinet
approval?). C11 step 1 allows "an executive decision", but steps 3 and 6 demand a numbered Y; C11 P8
depends on it. Close: state what Y is for a scheme and show one scheme trace.

**H-r2-11 (C09-2).** C09 `must_know[1].point`: "(heading 2202 10)". "Heading", "tariff item" and the
numbering system are undefined. Close: one clause naming the customs tariff / HSN classification.

**H-r2-12 (C09-3).** C09 `simplified_explanation`: "The Central Government then issues the notification
that sets the central rate." The State half of GST (SGST Act, State notification, how 20 + 20 = 40)
is never mentioned; no held SGST source. This is the most important missing link for the tax
proposal (also C11-3, C11-13, section 2(a)). Close: a held source for the State rate, or a sentence
stating what PIB 2163555 says, then one sentence on the split.

**H-r2-13 (C09-5), contradiction.** C09 `simplified_explanation`: "When several bodies must act, ask
which one goes first. That is the one your note should be addressed to." Conflicts with C02 ("every
body whose agreement the law requires") and C18 ("a member of the body that decides"). Close:
reconcile into one rule (e.g. address the first mover, copy every body whose agreement is required).

**H-r2-14 (C09-8, residual).** C09 `must_know[1].point` and `must_know[5].point`: "as amended by
01/2026 from 1 May 2026" names the amendment but not what it changed. Close: one clause from the held
notification on what 01/2026 did to Schedule III.

**H-r2-15 (C10-4).** C10 `must_know[7].point`: "leave predictions about who prevails to the process
theories of the next level". "Process theories" undefined; the next level is outside this book.
Close: name one process theory, or cut the pointer.

**H-r2-16 (C10-5), inconsistency.** C10 `definition.text`: "Lobbying is representing an
organisation's case before government ..." excludes individuals, yet plain terms list "Researchers,
which includes you" among actors who push. Close: widen the definition to "a person's or an
organisation's case", or say an individual's representation is not lobbying in this book's sense.

**H-r2-17 (C11-4), contradiction.** C11 `simplified_explanation`: "If you cannot name one, you have a
wish, not yet a proposal." C01 defines a proposal as any course of action someone wants a public body
to take, and its own example names no instrument. Close: align the two (e.g. "an untraced proposal").

**H-r2-18 (C11-6).** C11 `definition.text` step 4: "commencement on a notified date, and laying before
the legislature". Their order relative to each other and to Gazette notification is not given.
Close: one sentence: made and notified, in force on its date, laid after it is made (C07).

**H-r2-19 (C11-8), exercise.** C11 practice P3: "Then say which step the parts leave unanswered." The
parts leave steps 2, 4 and the carry-out half of 5. Close: "which steps", or supply the missing parts.

**H-r2-20 (C11-9), exercise.** C11 P2(a) quotes Union List entry 52 without tying it to C04's "FSS Act
puts the food industry under Union control". Close: one line in C04 or the P2 answer making the link.

**H-r2-21 (C11-10), exercise.** C11 P5 / `must_know[3].point`: COTPA never expanded; the National
Tobacco Control Programme and "State Food or Drug Administration" appear first here; what G.S.R.
687(E) did is not stated. Close: expand COTPA on first use, gloss the notification and the bodies.

**H-r2-22 (C11-11), exercise.** C11 P6: "clause (k) of sub-section (2) of section 92, read with section
23". Section 92(2) never quoted; "read with" undefined; "section 23" is ambiguous after C07 made it
the General Clauses Act. Close: one sentence on 92(2) as the list of subjects, gloss "read with", and
say this section 23 is the FSS Act's (labelling).

**H-r2-23 (C11-12), exercise.** C11 P8: "subject: agriculture, so the trace must find the State List
entry". No agriculture entry given; the ministry running NMEO never named; whether a scheme needs a
List entry under Article 282 unsettled. Close: give the entry, name the ministry, and settle the
Article 282 point (ties to H-r2-10).

**H-r2-24 (C11-14).** C11 P4 / C04: FSS Act section 16(1) "safe and wholesome food". "Wholesome" is
undefined, and whether sugar, salt or fat warnings fall under it decides steps 2 and 3. Close: quote
the section 3 definition (or say none exists) and state the unsettled point as a fork.
