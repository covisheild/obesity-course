# S47-R1 holes found by the compression pass, batch r1 (C01 to C06)

These are holes the original did not fill either, or errors. They go to the audit and the fixer.
Fields are as named in `<S>-final-prose.yml`. "As it stands" is the text the reader will see after
restoration.

## C01

**C01-1** · definition.text
- As it stands: "A common textbook model divides the policy process into four stages ..."
- What is wrong: no source is named for the model.
- To close: cite the textbook (and the quoted definition) in the form F3 demands.

**C01-4** · exercises (Ex4.2)
- As it stands: "The release of 18 December 2017: 'The Cabinet in its meeting held on 15th March, 2017 approved ...'"
- What is wrong: the issuer is not given. The nine-month gap is unexplained. "Cabinet" is used before C05 explains it.
- To close: name the issuing body (e.g. PIB) and note why the release date differs, or use a source dated at the meeting.

**C01-6** · exercises (Ex2.1, Ex2.4)
- As it stands: "since the collector spoke in favour of them" / "Health workers in the block"
- What is wrong: "collector" and "block" are never defined, and Ex2.1 turns on whether a collector holds power.
- To close: a one-line gloss of district collector and of block (the administrative unit), at first use or in the setting paragraph.

**C01-7** · definition.text / simplified_explanation
- As it stands: "Public policy is what government does ..." / "A programme is the organised activity through which an agency carries a policy out."
- What is wrong: "government", "a public body" and "an agency" are not distinguished. Plain terms switches to "an office".
- To close: use one term, or define how they relate.

**C01-9** · definition.text vs must_know[4].point
- As it stands: "Policy also includes ... a choice not to act." / "If a body with the power to act has been asked and has not acted, write that as its current policy."
- What is wrong: the step from "choice not to act" to "asked and not acted" is asserted. Ex2.3 does not say whether anyone asked.
- To close: one sentence linking a request left unanswered to a choice not to act, or amend Ex2.3 to say the body was asked.

## C02

**C02-1** · definition.text
- As it stands: "Minimum support prices (MSP), the prices at which the government will buy certain crops." / "Food regulations under the Food Safety and Standards (FSS) Act, 2006."
- What is wrong: these are verbless fragments that work as case headings.
- To close: make each a sentence, or mark it as a heading.

**C02-2 (remainder)** · definition.text
- As it stands: "For the kharif crops of marketing season 2026-27, ..."
- What is wrong: "kharif" and "marketing season" are undefined. Paddy as a kharif crop is not stated.
- To close: a gloss (kharif = the monsoon-sown crop season, which includes paddy).

**C02-3** · definition.text
- As it stands: "... the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) approved them."
- What is wrong: no power or provision is named for the CCEA, which breaks the section's own rule ("names the body that holds the decide job under a named power"). Its relation to the Cabinet is only partly given, by C05's restored rule 6 sentence.
- To close: name the instrument under which the CCEA decides (Transaction of Business Rules, rule 6 and the Cabinet committee schedule, under Article 77(3)) and cross-refer C05.

**C02-4** · definition.text, must_know[2].point
- As it stands: "with the previous approval of the Central Government"
- What is wrong: no section says which office acts as "the Central Government" (ministry, Minister, Cabinet). Every later trace needs it (C11 P4, gap report §2(a)).
- To close: a sourced sentence (General Clauses Act s.3(8), with Article 77 and the Allocation of Business Rules) that the department holding the subject acts as the Central Government. Place it in C02 or C05.

**C02-5** · definition.text, must_know[2].point
- As it stands: "after previous publication, by notification (section 92(1))"
- What is wrong: "previous publication" is unexplained until C07, but Ex2 asks for it.
- To close: a one-line gloss here, or a forward pointer to C07, or move Ex2's demand.

**C02-7** · definition.text
- As it stands: "The Central Advisory Committee advises the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India ..."
- What is wrong: no section is given, so "the place that says so" cannot be filled for the advise line.
- To close: cite FSS Act section 11.

**C03-7** · exercises (Ex4); the same in C05 Ex2

- What is wrong: no list of the department's proposals is ever given.
- To close: list the three proposals (tax, canteen rule, warning label) in C01's setting paragraph, or drop "third".

## C05

**C05-1** · definition.text
- As it stands: "Two orders of 14 January 1961 are those rules."
- What is wrong: "orders" and "rules" name the same documents, against C03's ladder. Rules made directly under Article 77(3), with no Act, are not placed on the ladder.
- To close: one sentence that these are rules made by the President under Article 77(3), issued as orders, and so sit directly under the Constitution.

**C05-4 (remainder)** · must_know[2].point
- As it stands: "The tax sits with the Department of Revenue"
- What is wrong: never placed in the Ministry of Finance, which C10 and C17 name.
- To close: "the Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance".

**C05-6** · definition.text
- As it stands: "Regulation of packaged commodities is with the Department of Consumer Affairs (item 6)."
- What is wrong: not told apart from food labelling under the FSS Act, so Ex2 cannot say whether Consumer Affairs owns part of a food warning label.
- To close: one sentence that packaged-commodity rules (Legal Metrology Act) cover declarations such as quantity and price, while food labelling standards are under the FSS Act.
