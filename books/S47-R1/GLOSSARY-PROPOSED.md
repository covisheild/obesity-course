# Proposed glossary rows · S47-R1 (Book 5)

Harmonised by the Task 2b reconciler on 2 October 2026 from the "Glossary rows" in
`draft-notes-b1.md` to `draft-notes-b7.md`. One row per term, one sense per term, alphabetical, same
columns as `prose/GLOSSARY.md`. Checked against `prose/GLOSSARY.md`, which was **not** edited: the
rows below go in at merge, after the audit has checked the plain words against the records.

Dropped because `prose/GLOSSARY.md` already has the term, and the record uses its sense:
`civil society organisation` (`S57-R1-C15`; C10's gloss was aligned to it), `conflict of interest`
(`S55-R1-C07`), `minimum support price` (`S37-R1-C09`), `instrument` (`B0-R0-C43`; the book uses
it only for a legal document and says "measure" for a tax, label or rule).

Needs the conductor, because it touches an existing row: **actor**. `prose/GLOSSARY.md` has
"actor (in a food system)" (`S37-R1-C01`). The row below is a second, qualified sense. Under the
glossary's own convention it would become sense (2) of one "actor" row, which changes an existing
row, a between-rounds job.

## Changes from the drafters' rows

- **b2's combined "Union List, State List, Concurrent List"** split into three rows, one term each.
- **b7's "campaigning (for policy), advocacy"** kept as one term, with "advocacy" named as its
  synonym in the gloss rather than as a second headword.
- **b7's "honest broker"** gave two numbered senses. The book teaches the Oliver and Cairney (2019)
  sense; the 2017 sense is a note in C17, so the row carries one sense.
- **b1's "proposal"** reworded to C01's own definition ("a course of action that someone wants a
  public body to take").
- **b1's "public policy"** reworded to C01's words ("what government does"), not "has decided and
  does".
- **b7's "policy advocate"**: C02 cites OpenStax's sentence only as a quote and glosses nothing, so
  C17 keeps the row (b7's own condition).
- **b3's "scheme"** kept beside b1's "programme": C01 teaches "programme", C08 "scheme", and the
  two glosses agree (a scheme is one kind of programme).
- **b5's "agenda setting"**: C01 first uses it as the first stage of the policy process. C12 now
  ties its definition to that stage, so the row has one sense with C01 as first use.
- **b6's "feasibility (of a policy option)"** kept qualified: it is a different sense from the
  existing "feasible (question)".
- **"Pre-legislative Consultation Policy"**: "senior civil servants" replaced by "the Committee of
  Secretaries", the words C06 and C07 use.
- **"food environment" and "food system"** are not proposed: both are existing rows from S37-R1.
  The book's "food-environment frame" (C13 to C15) and "food-system frame" (C14, C15, the Eat
  Right India page) use them in those senses.
