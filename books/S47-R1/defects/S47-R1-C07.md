1. **error** · `exercises[2].answer` (the fix line); `must_know[1].point`; `exercises[3].answer`
   - Claim: Ex3: "Send our objection before that date. FSSAI must consider it, and so must the Central
     Government, whose approval section 92 requires." must_know[1]: the three phrases tell you
     "which two bodies must hear you". Ex4: "'By notification' tells you the final text will appear
     in the Gazette, and that Gazette publication settles that it was duly made."
   - Source: General Clauses Act s.23 applies where a power "to make rules or bye-laws" is given
     subject to previous publication. FSSAI makes **regulations** under s.92; no held source says s.23
     governs them, and the FSS Act itself (s.92(1)) says only "after previous publication". The duty to
     consider objections, and the approving authority's duty, are therefore not established for an
     FSSAI regulation. s.23(5)'s "conclusive proof" is limited to a rule or bye-law made "after
     previous publication", not to anything made "by notification".
   - Fix: Ex3: "Send our objection before that date, to FSSAI, and copy the Central Government, whose
     approval section 92 requires." must_know[1]: "... whether a draft will appear, and which bodies
     act before the final text." Ex4: "... the final text will appear in the Gazette. For a rule made
     after previous publication, section 23 of the General Clauses Act makes that publication
     conclusive proof it was duly made."
   - Recurring (SELFCHECK 4: a claim stronger than its source).
   Fixed: Ex3 fix line now sends the objection to FSSAI and copies the Central Government; must_know[1] ends "which bodies act before the final text"; Ex4 confines conclusive proof to a rule made after previous publication (GCA s.23(5)).
   Verified: closed

   - Source: s.92(1) lists "previous approval" before "after previous publication", s.94(1) the other way
     round. GCA s.23(4) (for rules) makes the approving authority consider objections received before
     the notice date, so approval comes after the objections close. Ex2 asks for steps "in order".
   - Fix: one sentence after the three conditions: "The order of the words is not the order of the

   Fixed: definition adds that the order of words is not the order of steps, the draft first, the approving authority acting after the notice date because s.23(4) makes it consider objections received before it; for an FSSAI regulation the FSS Act does not set the order in words.
   Verified: closed
