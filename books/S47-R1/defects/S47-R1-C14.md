1. **error** · `must_know[6].point`, "Framing obesity as a disability or a disease is not neutral ... the
   disability account went with support for protection from discrimination and for treatment, and
   with less support for most other measures. Choose a disease frame knowing which part of the menu
   it opens."; `simplified_explanation` bullet 3, "If obesity is a disease, the sensible measures are
   treatment, and protection from unfair treatment."; `exercises[2].answer`, "If it is a disease, the
   list is treatment and protection from discrimination."
   - Barry tested a disability metaphor, not a disease frame. C13's table puts the disease frame on
     Saguy and Riley and Kwan, never on Barry. C14 then carries Barry's disability results over to
     "disease" in three places. Second, "less support for most other measures" is not in the quote
     cited (which says disability was "significantly associated with only three policies"). The
     paper is inconsistent here: its Discussion says disability believers were "generally less
     supportive", but its Results say that for compensatory and redistributive policies "all the mid-
     and low-level blame metaphors (i.e., obesity as disability, ...) were positively and
     significantly associated with policy support", and negatively for price-raising ones.
   - Fix: must_know[6]: "In Barry's survey, the disability account went with support for
     anti-discrimination protection and treatment funding, and with opposition to higher insurance
     premiums; for other single measures it predicted nothing either way. The survey did not test a
     disease frame." Bullet 3: "If obesity is a disability or a disease, the sensible measures are
     treatment and protection from unfair treatment" and add "(the survey tested the disability
     account only)" where it is cited. Teaching answer the same.
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 4a and 4b: a finding used outside what was measured).
   Fixed: must_know[6] replaced with the auditor's wording (disability account: anti-discrimination protection, treatment funding, opposition to higher premiums; nothing either way for other single measures; disease frame not tested). Plain-terms bullet now reads "a disability or a disease", and the survey sentence adds "(it tested a disability account, not a disease frame)". Teaching answer changed to match, with "the survey below tested the disability account only".
   Verified: closed
