1. **error** · `must_know[4].point`
   - Claim: "Poshan 2.0 delivers meals that sections 4, 5 and 6 of the National Food Security Act make a
     right. Changing the right needs Parliament to amend the Act."
   - Source: NFSA s.37(1): "If the Central Government is satisfied that it is necessary or expedient so
     to do, it may, by notification, amend Schedule I or Schedule II ...". Schedule II holds the
     "nutritional standards" that the meals in ss.4(a), 5(1) and 6 must meet. So the meal entitlement
     needs Parliament, but the standards the meal must meet can be changed by the Central Government
     by notification (laid before Parliament, s.37(2)). s.4 also opens "Subject to such schemes as may
     be framed by the Central Government".
   - Fix: "Changing the entitlement itself needs Parliament to amend the Act. The nutritional standards
     in Schedule II can be changed by Central Government notification (section 37)." Add an `nfsa_2013`
     reference quoting s.37(1).
   - Recurring (SELFCHECK 4: a claim stronger than its source).

4. **gap** · `definition.text` and `simplified_explanation` (from compression hole H-r2-10, C08-8, shared
   with C11)
   - Claim: a scheme rests on "a Cabinet approval and a budget"; the numbered provision under which the
     Union runs a scheme is never named, though C11 steps 3 and 6 ask for one.
   - Source: the held Constitution has Article 73(1): "the executive power of the Union shall extend—
     (a) to the matters with respect to which Parliament has power to make laws"; and Article 282 for
     grants outside those matters (already cited). Which provision a given scheme relies on is not
     stated in either held scheme text (the NMEO release, the Poshan guidelines); Poshan's guidelines
     instead name NFSA ss.4(a), 5(1)(a), 6 and 7.
   - Fix: one sentence: "A Union scheme is an act of the Union's executive power (Article 73), and its
     money may go to the States as grants under Article 282. Its guidelines may also name an Act, as
     Poshan 2.0's do." Then C11 can take Y = Article 73 / 282 or the named Act. Quote 73(1).
   Disposition (Fixed / Partly / Rejected):
   Verified:
