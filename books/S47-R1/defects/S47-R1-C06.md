2. **error** · `definition.text`, paragraph 3
   - Claim: "a Bill may start in either House, unless it is a Money Bill (Article 107(1))."
   - Source: Article 107(1) is "Subject to the provisions of articles 109 and 117". Article 117(1):
     a Bill making provision for any matter in Article 110(1)(a) to (f) "shall not be introduced in
     the Council of States". The booklet (IX, "Financial Bills—Category-I") says the same. So a tax
     Bill that also carries other provisions (not a Money Bill) still cannot start in the Rajya Sabha.
   - Fix: "unless it is a Money Bill, or another Bill that deals with tax or the other matters in
     Article 110(1) (Articles 107(1), 109(1) and 117(1))". Add a constitution reference quoting
     117(1).
   - Recurring (SELFCHECK 4: a claim stronger than its source).
   Fixed: definition paragraph 3 now excepts a Money Bill and any other Bill dealing with the matters in Article 110(1)(a) to (f) (Articles 107(1), 109(1) and 117(1)); added a reference quoting Article 117(1) ("... shall not be introduced in the Council of States").
