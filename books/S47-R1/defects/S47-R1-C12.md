1. **error** · `definition.text`, "In the form most used in health policy research, the choice serves
   four functions."
   - Koon and colleagues give Entman's four functions as one classification among several ("frames
     can be classified based on whether they define, diagnose, judge or prescribe (Entman 1993).
     Similarly, other scholars suggest ..."), and their Discussion reports "a lack of consensus ...
     across disciplines". Nothing in the held review says Entman's form is the most used. The claim
     is not in the source.
   - Fix: "In one widely cited form, Entman's (1993), the choice serves four functions ..." or simply
     "In Entman's (1993) account, as reported by Koon, Hawkins and Mayhew ...".
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 1 and 4).

   - Verified:

2. **style** · `definition.text`, "Agenda setting is the choice of which issues get attention at all,
   the part of the policy process that comes first."; `simplified_explanation`, "before any frame is
   chosen".
   - C01's textbook model defines agenda setting as "made up of problem identification and the
     specifying of alternative solutions", which includes choosing remedies, the fourth framing
     function. C12 narrows agenda setting to choosing the topic and places it before any frame. A
     reader holding C01's definition meets a different one here.
   - Fix: "Agenda setting, the first stage of the policy process in the model of the first section,
     starts with the choice of which issues get attention at all." Keep "before any frame is chosen"
     only for that choice of topic.
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 7: one term for one thing across sections).
   - Fixed/Partly/Rejected:
   - Verified:
