     central notification gives the Union's share."

     Standard Rate of 18% and a Merit Rate of 5%; a special de-merit rate of 40% for a select few goods

     any section having given them.
   - Fix: Step 5: "The release calls 40% 'a special de-merit rate' of GST, beside a Standard Rate of
     18% and a Merit Rate of 5%. It does not split it between Union and States. The central
     notification sets central tax at 20%. What each State issued is not among this book's sources."
     Add a `pib_2163555` reference quoting the rate-structure line. This also gives C14/C15 their
     first use (40% the highest of the three rates).
     H-r2-12's request for a held State-rate source is withdrawn: none is held and the brief forbids
     going beyond PIB; Step 5 and the boundary now state the limit honestly. Partly closed.
   Disposition (Fixed / Partly / Rejected):
