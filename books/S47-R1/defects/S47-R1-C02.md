2. **gap** · `definition.text`, paragraph 4 (`references[7]`, Article 32(2))
   - Claim: "Under Articles 32 and 226 of the Constitution, the Supreme Court and the High Courts may
     issue directions, orders or writs."
   - Source: Article 32(2) gives the power "for the enforcement of any of the rights conferred by this
     Part" (fundamental rights only). Article 226(1) adds "and for any other purpose". The sentence
     gives the two articles equal reach.
   - Fix: "The Supreme Court may issue directions, orders or writs to enforce fundamental rights
     (Article 32); a High Court may issue them for those rights and for any other purpose (Article
     226)."
   - Recurring (SELFCHECK 4: claim stronger than its source).

   Verified:
