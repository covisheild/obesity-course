Written at Task 1 (PIPELINE.md), after the inventory, on 2026-10-01. The map was audited once as a
whole; this checks the rung at its own level against three standards opened now. Only items a rung-1

**Gaps proposed to Harsh** (in the source-gate message; never added without his word):

1. **SWOT analysis of a policy** (NMC Health Policy objective v). Not in the map at any S47 rung. The
   course's position so far is that a policy is analysed by instrument, authority, evidence and
   interests, which C11 and C16–C17 teach; SWOT is a management checklist. Proposal: no amendment, or at
   most a one-line mention in S47-R2 so an examination question does not surprise the reader. Harsh
   decides.
2. **Power as an analytic concept** (Buse et al. ch. 2; Gilson reader §5). S47-R1 covers actors and
   interests (C10) and S39 covers corporate political activity, but no S47 rung names power itself
   (visible, hidden and agenda-setting power; where a body's power comes from beyond law). Proposal: an
   amendment to S47-R2 P1 adding "power, and the forms in which it is exercised", which Kingdon and the
   advocacy coalition framework assume. Not added to this inventory.
