1. **error** · `simplified_explanation`, "It does this in two rounds, one for the kharif crops and one
   for the rabi crops."
   - Two rounds cover 14 kharif and 6 rabi crops, 20 of the 22. The held backgrounder's own table
     lists jute and copra separately, under "COMMERCIAL CROPS", with their own MSPs (jute 5,650,
     milling copra 11,582).
   - Fix: "Most are announced in two rounds, one for the kharif crops and one for the rabi crops;
     jute and copra are listed apart, as commercial crops."
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 1 and 4).
   - Fixer: applied the suggested sentence in `simplified_explanation` (backgrounder table lists Jute and Copra under "COMMERCIAL CROPS").

2. **error** · `must_know[1]`, "MSP is announced for many crops. Procurement is concentrated in one."
   (from compression hole 23, residual; confirmed).
   - The evidence is kharif only. Wheat, a rabi crop, is bought at MSP in bulk: 300.35 lakh tonnes in
     rabi marketing season 2025-26, which illustration 1 itself gives. Across all crops the claim is
     false.
   - Fix: "Among the kharif crops, procurement is concentrated in one."
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 4a: a finding for one group stated for all).
   - Fixer: must_know[1] last sentence now "Among the kharif crops, procurement is concentrated in one."

3. **gap** · `definition.text`, "Section 2(10) of the National Food Security Act, 2013 defines the
   minimum support price (MSP)", then MSP for moong, cotton and 22 crops (also compression hole H21,
   batch r3, raised against C16; confirmed as bearing on C09).
   - Section 2(10) defines MSP as the price "at which foodgrains are procured ... for the central
     pool"; foodgrains are rice, wheat and coarse grains (section 2(5)). For pulses, oilseeds, cotton
     and jute the Act says nothing; their MSP rests only on the annual decision. The reader cannot
     tell, and C16's table ("a decision announced each season") then reads as a contradiction.
   - Fix: add after the first paragraph: "The Act's definition is for foodgrains bought into the
     central pool. It does not set any price. Each season's MSP, for all 22 crops, rests on a
     Cabinet decision." C16's fixer then only needs a matching phrase.
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 7: one book, one account of the same thing).
   - Fixer: added the three suggested sentences as a new paragraph after the first (s. 2(5) confirmed: rice, wheat or coarse grains).

4. **gap** · `definition.text`, "also on CACP's recommendation".
   - CACP is used in the definition before the simplified explanation expands it, and "also" points
     at a recommendation the definition never mentions (the MSP paragraph names only the CCEA).
   - Fix: in the MSP paragraph, "The Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP) recommends
     it, and the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) decides it." (reference 3 already
     quotes the backgrounder for the recommendation).
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 14; acronym before its expansion, claude.md §10 rule 3).
   - Fixer: MSP paragraph now opens "The Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP) recommends the MSP, and the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) decides it."; "also on CACP's recommendation" now has its antecedent.

5. **gap** · `must_know[6]`, "the CACP's all-India weighted average, including imputed family labour"
   (from compression hole 21, residual; confirmed).
   - Neither "weighted" nor "imputed" is explained. The held releases say "All-India weighted
     average" and never say what the average is weighted by; the footnote lists what the cost counts,
     ending "imputed value of family labour".
   - Fix: "The cost is an all-India average across States, weighted in a way the releases do not
     state. It counts cash costs and puts a value on the family's own unpaid work ('imputed family
     labour')."
   - Fixer: must_know[6] now "an all-India average, weighted in a way the releases do not state. It counts paid-out costs and puts a value on the family's own unpaid work ('imputed family labour')." ("across States" dropped: no held text says it; "cash" changed to the footnote's "paid-out").
