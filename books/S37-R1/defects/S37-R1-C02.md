1. **Illustration 2, "Every row here is food loss, because it all happens before the shop."**
   (from compression hole H9, corrected)
   - **What the source says:** the release lists the stages NABCONS assessed, ending "...storage
     at farm level and Godown, wholesalers, retailers, processing unit and transport at market
     level" (`pib_2151371.txt`, the reply). So the "market level" column includes retailers.
     UNEP's food loss stops "up to, and excluding, the retail level"; loss at retail is UNEP's
     food *waste*. The sentence puts part of the market-level column on the wrong side of the
     line the section has just taught.
   - **Smallest fix:** "The farm-operations column is food loss in UNEP's sense. The market-level
     column is not cleanly either: the release says it covers wholesalers, retailers, processing
     units and transport, and retail is where UNEP's waste begins." Add the stage sentence as a
     reference with that quote.
   - **Tag:** error. **Recurring** (SELFCHECK 4: a conclusion the source does not license).
