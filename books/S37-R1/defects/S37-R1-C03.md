# Audit defects · S37-R1-C03 · A packaged food is many chains meeting

Task 3 audit, 25 September 2026 (auditor a1). All eleven `quote` fields are in
`fssai_labelling_2020.txt` (compendium Version-VIII, 09.09.2025) word for word. By hand: the
block quote of 5(6)(a) in illustration 1 and the 5 per cent proviso in illustration 2's numbers
are in the file. The class-title table (5(2)(d) proviso) was read row by row: serial 1 edible
vegetable oil, 8 spices ("Spice and condiments, herbs or mixed spices/condiments as appropriate"),
11 sucrose "Sugar", 15 milk "Milk solids (source may also be given)". 5(2)(e) defines a compound
ingredient; 5(2)(g) has two triggers and six exemptions; 5(7)(b) and 5(12)(a) say what the record
says. Not quantitative. No figure; the `figure_note` gives a reason. No quote edited.

"Recurring" as defined in the C01 file.

1. **`simplified_explanation`, "The ingredients go in order of weight when the food was made,
   heaviest first."** (from compression hole H20)
   - **What the source says:** confirmed. 5(2)(b): "by weight or volume, as the case may be". The
     plain terms drop volume, so for a drink "heaviest first" is not the rule.
   - **Smallest fix:** "in order of weight when the food was made, heaviest first (by volume for
     some liquid foods)."
   - **Tag:** gap.

2. **`definition.text`, "only where regulation 5(2)(g) requires one, for example where the label
   emphasises that ingredient"; illustration 2's `analogy_breaks_when`, "gives no amounts, unless
   the label emphasises an ingredient".** (from compression hole H15)
   - **What the source says:** confirmed, and the analogy line is wrong as worded. 5(2)(g) has a
     second trigger: an ingredient "not within the name of the food but ... essential to
     characterise the food and ... expected to be present", where leaving the percentage out
     would mislead. The analogy line names only the first as if it were the only one.
   - **Smallest fix:** definition: "...requires one: where the label emphasises that ingredient in
     words or pictures, or where the ingredient characterises the food and leaving its percentage
     out would mislead." Analogy: "unless regulation 5(2)(g) requires a percentage, as it does for
     an ingredient the label emphasises."
   - **Tag:** gap.

3. **Exercise 1, "Which ingredient hides more than one chain behind a single name, and which rule
   lets it?"** (from compression hole H21)
   - **What the source says:** confirmed. The spices class title (serial 8) is in the regulation,
     but the section teaches only Sugar and Milk solids (and the oil row). The answer supplies
     the rule the text never gave.
   - **Smallest fix:** add to the definition's class-title paragraph: "Spices, herbs and
     condiments may be declared together under one class title."
   - **Tag:** gap. **Recurring** (SELFCHECK 14).

4. **Illustration 2 and `must_know[1]`, "Sugar and invert sugar syrup are listed as two
   lines".** (from compression hole H17)
   - **What the record says:** confirmed. Invert sugar syrup is never glossed, and that it is a
     sugar is assumed by the must-know's "Add up an ingredient that appears under more than one
     name". No held file defines it.
   - **Smallest fix:** in illustration 2, after the list: "Invert sugar syrup is sugar split into
     its two simpler sugars and sold as a syrup." Mark it as the book's gloss (no held source), or
     hold a source for it.
   - **Tag:** gap. **Recurring** (SELFCHECK 14).

5. **`definition.text` and illustration 1, "only the manufacturer's licence number must
   appear".** (from compression hole H18)
   - **What the record says:** confirmed. "Licence" is never introduced: who issues it, and what it
     licenses.
   - **Smallest fix:** at first use: "the FSSAI licence number — the number of the licence FSSAI
     issues to a food business". 5(7)(a)'s "license number under the Act" is the quote.
   - **Tag:** gap. **Recurring** (SELFCHECK 14).

6. **`definition.text`, "milk products as 'Milk solids', with the source optional".** (from
   compression hole H16)
   - **What the source says:** confirmed as unclear; the row reads "Milk solids (source may also
     be given)" against "All milk and milk products derived solely from milk". "Source" there is
     the animal or product the solids came from.
   - **Smallest fix:** "...as 'Milk solids'; the label may also say where the solids came from, but
     need not."
   - **Tag:** style.

7. **`must_know[2]`, "the label shows the maker's licence number, not its name".**
   - **What the source says:** 5(6)(a) requires the brand owner's name and address; 5(7)(b) adds
     the maker's licence number. Nothing forbids the maker's name. "Not its name" reads as a rule.
   - **Smallest fix:** "the label must show the maker's licence number; it need not show its
     name."
   - **Tag:** style.
