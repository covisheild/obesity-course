1. **gap** · `definition.text`, "Households under the Antyodaya Anna Yojana are entitled instead to
   thirty-five kilograms per household per month."; figure caption (from compression hole 41;
   confirmed, retagged gap).
   - The proviso reads "the households covered under Antyodaya Anna Yojana shall, to such extent as
     may be specified by the Central Government for each State in the said scheme, be entitled to
     thirty-five kilograms". The qualifier fixes how many households are Antyodaya in each State,
     not the 35 kg, so "35 kg whatever its size" in the figure is right. But the definition drops a
     clause that Book 0 A5 and B2 carry, and section 10(1)(a) depends on it ("to the extent specified
     under sub-section (1) of section 3").
   - Fix: "Households under the Antyodaya Anna Yojana, as many in each State as the Central
     Government specifies, are entitled instead to thirty-five kilograms per household per month."
     Extend reference 2's quote to begin at "to such extent as may be specified".
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 7: contradicts an earlier book by omission).
Fixer: definition now says Antyodaya households are "as many in each State as the Central Government specifies"; reference 2's quote extended to begin at "to such extent as may be specified" (checked in nfsa_2013.txt).

2. **gap** · `definition.text`, "Under section 10 the State Government identifies the Antyodaya
   households and the priority households within it."; Exercise 1 answer, "The Central Government
   fixes how many people each State covers (section 9)."
   - "Within it" has no clear referent. Section 10 says "within the number of persons determined
     under section 9". Section 9 is quoted in reference 5 but never appears in the reader's text, yet
     the Exercise 1 answer and `must_know[5]` ("not the share covered in any one State") rely on it.
   - Fix: "Under section 9 the Central Government fixes how many people each State covers. Under
     section 10 the State Government picks the Antyodaya and priority households within that number."
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 14: an answer uses what the text never taught).
Fixer: replaced the sentence with the section 9 / section 10 pair as proposed.

3. **gap** · illustration 2 table, "a price of at most Rs 3, 2 and 1 a kg, then at most the MSP | the
   Act, Schedule I | the Central Government, within the Schedule"; "Nothing in the Act says zero."
   - Section 37(1) of the held Act lets the Central Government "by notification, amend Schedule I or
     Schedule II", with each notification laid before Parliament (37(2)). So the Schedule I ceiling
     itself can be changed without an Act of Parliament. The table says only "within the Schedule".
     Also, a price of zero is "not exceeding" the MSP, so zero is within Schedule I's power. As
     written, "Nothing in the Act says zero" reads as if zero were outside the Act. For rice, the
     ceiling is "the derived minimum support price", not the MSP.
   - Fix: table cell, "the Central Government, within the Schedule, or by amending it under section
     37"; and after "Nothing in the Act says zero": "Zero is allowed, because it does not exceed the
     ceiling, but the Act does not require it." Write "at most the MSP (for rice, the derived MSP)".
     Add a reference to section 37 with its quote.
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 1: a table cell that says less than the instrument it cites).
Fixer: prose now "up to the minimum support price (MSP), or for rice the derived MSP. The Act allows zero, but does not require it." (replacing "Nothing in the Act says zero"); table cell says "(derived MSP for rice)" and "...within the Schedule, or by amending it under section 37"; added section 37(1) reference, quote checked in nfsa_2013.txt.

5. **style** · `simplified_explanation`, "a set quantity of grain each month has been a legal right for
   most Indians".
   - Section 3(2) sets ceilings ("up to") of 75 per cent rural and 50 per cent urban, and the section
     itself warns that these are ceilings. "Most" is a reading of the ceilings as coverage.
   - Fix: "a legal right for up to three-quarters of rural and half of urban Indians".
Fixer: applied; also Exercise 3 answer "to most households" changed to "to priority households" (same reading of the ceilings).
