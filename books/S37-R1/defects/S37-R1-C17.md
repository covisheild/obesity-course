1. **`definition.text`: the food-environment elements, and "It then moves no element of the food
   environment"** (from compression hole r3-H22; brief's C05/C17 consistency check)
   - Confirmed. C05, from HLPE 2017 s.1.2.2, names the element "promotion, advertising and
     information". C17 shortens it to "promotion" and then says an awareness campaign "moves no
     element". A campaign is information, so on C05's list it moves one. Exercise 1(b) then files a
     front-of-pack warning under "promotion and information", which contradicts the definition's
     own four names.
   - Fix: name the element as C05 does ("promotion, advertising and information"), and replace "It
     then moves no element of the food environment" with "It changes nothing on offer: the food, its
     price and where it is sold stay as they were; at most it adds information." Carry "nothing on
     offer" (already the tables' wording) through must_know[0] and the retrieval answer.
   - **error** · **recurring** (SELFCHECK 7).
