on `check/SELFCHECK.md` or appears in `books/B0/defects/`, `books/S01-R1/defects/` or
`books/S02-R1/defects/`.

**Quotes.** All 9 `definition.references` quotes and all 5 `illustration.numbers` quotes found
verbatim (`dfpd_pds.txt`, `econ_survey_tab_1_19.txt`, `nfsa_2013.txt`); each number quote states its
number. Table 1.19 columns read against the file's own notes: population, gross production, net
production, net imports, change in stocks, net availability, kg a year, g a day. Nothing rests on
the withdrawn `food_corporations_act_1964`: the Act is named only as "an Act of 1964", from the
DFPD report's own sentence, and paragraph 3.64 replaces the old section 13 source. Schedule II of
the NFSA as held has only Calories (Kcal) and Protein (g) columns, so "no upper limit on energy,
sugar, fat or salt" holds. No quote was edited.

correct.

**Figures.** `net-imports` plots −22,347, −4,682, −3,323 and 1,733 from the record's table, bars

1. **gap** · `definition.text`, "The measure these institutions are judged by is also a quantity. For
   2022-23 it gives net imports of cereals ..." (from compression hole 11; confirmed).
   - "It" has no antecedent (the table is never named in the definition), and "judged by" is the
     author's framing: no held source says the institutions are judged by Table 1.19. The same
     framing recurs in `must_know[0]` ("judged by tonnes of grain") and in "The Department states
     the FCI's job today in quantities of grain", though paragraph 3.64 lists functions
     (procurement, distribution, buffer stock), not quantities.
   - Fix: "The national scoreboard is also a quantity. The Economic Survey 2025-26's Table 1.19
     gives net imports of cereals for 2022-23 of minus 30,352 thousand tonnes ..."; and "The
     Department describes the FCI's job today in terms of grain."
   - Recurring: yes (a pronoun whose referent was cut, S02-R1-C05 item 4; SELFCHECK 4).

2. **error** · `illustration.body`, "The system built to end a grain shortage now runs a grain
   surplus."; `must_know[0]`, "by that measure it succeeded" (from compression hole 13; confirmed,
   and worse than the hole says).
   - "Surplus" is undefined, and the table the section quotes contradicts it for this year: the
     cereals row shows a change in stocks of −18,155 thousand tonnes in 2022-23 (the quote in
     reference 6 carries it). Stocks fell by about 18 million tonnes in the year cereal net exports
     were about 30 million, so much of the export came out of stocks. One year's net export is not
     evidence of a production surplus, and the analogy's own caveat ("a single year of trade can
     swing") is the reason.
   - Fix: "The system built to end a grain shortage now exports more cereal than it imports. In
     2022-23 it also drew its cereal stocks down by 18,155 thousand tonnes." Keep must_know[0]'s
     "succeeded" only as "by that measure it has ended the shortage it was built for".
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 4: a conclusion the source does not license).

3. **gap** · `must_know[4]`, "The PDS was never meant to be a whole diet ... Do not judge the ration as
   if it had to supply everything" (from compression hole 12, residual; confirmed).
   - "Ration" is not defined, and nothing in C08 says the ration is rice and wheat, although Ex 1
     ("Why does the ration give rice and wheat") assumes it. The procurement-to-ration link comes
     only in C09 and C11.
   - Fix: add to `simplified_explanation`, after the PDS sentence: "What a ration shop hands out is a
     monthly quantity of grain, mainly rice and wheat." Source it to the DFPD Annual Report 2025-26
     allocation table for TPDS (rice 361.11, wheat 184.36, nutri-cereals 8.64 lakh tonnes, in
     `dfpd_pds`). No pointer forward to a later section: §10 rule 1.
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 14: a term used before it is explained).

4. **style** · `definition.text`, "the Department of Food and Public Distribution's own account" (from
   compression hole H13, cross-file, batch r3; confirmed for C08).
   - This is the book's first use of the full name; C14 later uses "DFPD" unexpanded.
   - Fix: "the Department of Food and Public Distribution (DFPD)'s own account" here, so C14's use
     resolves.
   - Recurring: yes (SELFCHECK 14; acronyms expanded at first use, claude.md §10 rule 3).

5. **style** · `simplified_explanation`, "coarse grains, the cereals other than those two".
   - The gloss is unsourced. The nearest held words are the Poshan 2.0 guidelines' "millets (coarse
     grains)" and Table 1.19's "other cereals". It is close enough for a reader, but it is the
     book's definition of a term the NFSA uses (C11).
   - Fix: "coarse grains, the millets and other cereals besides those two", or leave it and add
     "(Table 1.19 calls them other cereals)".

6. **withdrawn** · compression hole 14, "There is no practice set."
   - C08 is not quantitative in the inventory or the draft brief (quantitative: C04, C06, C07, C10,
     C13), carries no `quantitative: true`, and its one sum is worked in the illustration and drawn
     in the figure. No drill set is required.
