1. **`illustration.body`: invert sugar syrup skipped** (from compression hole r3-H26)
   - Confirmed. "Take the first three ingredients, and milk solids and salt as well" skips the fourth
     ingredient; C03 teaches that sugar appears "twice, as 'Sugar' and as 'Invert sugar syrup'", and
     Exercise 1 asks for the first four.
   - Fix: "Take the first four; invert sugar syrup is sugar under another name and traces to the same
     sugar crop" and add it to the sugar row.
   - **error** (contradicts C03 and the exercise).

2. **Table, palm oil row: "tools checked"** (from compression hole r3-H27)
   - Confirmed. The 5% is the basic duty on *crude* palm oil. Release 2314297 says the Government
     "simultaneously reduced the applicable BCD on the respective refined edible oils while
     maintaining an import duty differential of 19.25%", but gives no refined rate; a biscuit's oil
     may be refined.
   - Fix: "crude palm oil duty 5% checked; the refined-oil rate is not stated in the release; whether
     either applies to this oil unknown".
   - **error** · **recurring** (SELFCHECK 4a: a figure for one form applied to another).

3. **Table, sugar row: "recovery" and "sugar season" undefined** (from compression hole r3-H28)
   - Confirmed. The quote itself gives the season "(October - September)"; the release's "basic
     recovery rate of 10.25%" is not explained.
   - Fix: "sugar season 2026-27 (October to September) ... at a basic recovery rate of 10.25%, the
     share of sugar recovered from the cane".
   - **gap** · **recurring** (SELFCHECK 14).

4. **"The factory as unknown unless the pack names it" and the licence number** (from compression hole r3-H29)
   - Confirmed, and the held text closes it. fssai_labelling_2020 5(7)(b): "the license number of
     the manufacturer or marketer or packer or bottler ... if different from the brand owner, shall
     also be displayed on the label." So the maker's licence number is seen on the pack even when its
     name is not (which is what C03 says).
   - Fix: "The pack also carries the maker's FSSAI licence number if it differs from the brand owner
     (regulation 5(7)(b)); mark it seen. This book does not look the number up."
   - **gap**.
