1. **error** · practice 18 (level 10) answer, "If each target was set from the person's own
   baseline, the two are not independent. So the reported 784 is neither confirmed nor shown
   wrong by this check."
   - A target set from the baseline makes the two rise and fall together. That makes the spread
     of baseline minus target *less* than the adding rule's 747, not more. A reported 784 would
     need the two to move in opposite directions (a covariance of about −28,300, a correlation of
     about −0.1). So the dependence the answer names cannot explain 784. The answer's main point
     (the means show the column is not baseline minus target) stands.
   - Fix: after "the two are not independent.", add "That kind of link would pull the spread below
     747, not push it above. So dependence of that kind cannot explain the 784 either; the means
     have already shown the column was made some other way."
   - Recurring: yes (a bound or direction reversed; SELFCHECK 13).
   - Fixer: added the two sentences after "the two are not independent." as given (checked in Python: 784 would need a covariance of about −28,300, correlation about −0.10).
