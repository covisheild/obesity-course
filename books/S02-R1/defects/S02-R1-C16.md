1. **error** · `definition.text`: the rule says each line comes from the one line before it.
   - Claim: "a chain of equations in which each line follows from the line before it by one move".
   - What the section does: substitution draws on earlier lines as well. P2's third line uses lines
     1 and 2. The Thomas rebuild's line 3 puts line 2 into line 1. The Polidori rebuild's line 4
     puts lines 2 and 3 into line 1. As stated, the rule marks all of these as skips.
   - Fix: "each line follows from lines above it by one move that can be named". Substitute's gloss
     can then say "replace a symbol by something an earlier line states is equal to it".

2. **error** · `illustration.body`: Polidori's Equation 1 is misquoted and its inputs overstated.
   - Claim: "Polidori and colleagues work out the change in each person's energy intake from their
     weights alone, with their Equation 1", followed by `ΔEI = ρ × dW/dt + ε × (W − W0)`.
   - Source: the MathML Equation 1 has two more terms, (Δδ/(1 − β))BW_0 and UGE, and it is written
     in BW_i. The paper's inputs also include the urinary glucose, "assumed to be ~90 g/day", so the
     calculation does not use weights alone. "Each subject" is right ("for each subject").
   - Fix: "... work out the change in each person's energy intake from their weights, with their
     Equation 1. Its first two terms, in this book's symbols (W for their BW), are:". Then add
     "Its other terms, for activity and for glucose lost in the urine, come back in problem 9."
     Drop "alone".
   - **recurring** (a source's equation or figure restated in a changed form, as B0 and S01
     "as printed" defects).
