3. **error** · `illustration.body`, "The ratio is what the curve is multiplied by over one step, so
   it is e^(-k) for a step of one year." (from compression hole A-C04-4; confirmed).
   - The curve 0.0, 50.0, 75.0, 87.5 is not multiplied by 0.5. What is multiplied by 0.5 each year
     is the gap to the ceiling (100, 50, 25, 12.5) and each year's gain (50.0, 25.0, 12.5). Book 0
     C20 defines "exponential" as the output itself being multiplied, so the sentence also teaches
     that this saturating curve is exponential.
   - Fix: "The ratio is what the gap to the ceiling, and each year's gain, is multiplied by over one
     step, so it is e^(-k) for a step of one year." (The cut definition sentence in
     `compress/S02-R1-C04-original.md` says it correctly: "Read at equal steps of time h, the gap is
     multiplied by the same factor, e^(-kh), at every step, and so is each successive change.")

4. **error** · `must_know[3].point`, "To test whether a claimed curve is exponential, divide its
   rate by its value at two or more times." (from compression hole A-C04-5; confirmed).
   - For W = L + (W0 − L) e^(−kt), dW/dt ÷ W is not constant; dW/dt ÷ (W − L) is (−k). The section's
     own example is a curve levelling at a level, so the test as written fails on it.
   - Fix: "divide its rate by its value, or, for a curve levelling off at L, by its gap to L, at two
     or more times."

5. **error** · `simplified_explanation`, "The number k is the rate constant, the rate divided by the
   amount.", against `definition.text`, "Where the quantity shrinks, write y = A e^(-kt) with k
   above zero." (from compression hole A-C04-8; confirmed).
   - For A e^(−kt) the rate is −k A e^(−kt), so rate ÷ amount = −k, a negative number, while k is
     declared positive. The text never states the rate of e^(−kt) at all; the reader must substitute
     −k into the e^(kt) rule unaided.
   - Fix: after "Its unit is one over time", add: "For a quantity that shrinks, written A e^(-kt)
     with k above zero, the rate is -k times the amount, so the rate divided by the amount is -k."
   - Notation (book-wide): C04 fixes decay as e^(−kt), k > 0, as OpenStax §6.8 does. C06
     `must_know[1]` ("where k is negative", item in C06's list) and C07 ("k is minus 1 divided by
     385.809") use the other convention. The fixers should hold every section to C04's.
