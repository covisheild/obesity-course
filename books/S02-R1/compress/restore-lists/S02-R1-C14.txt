# B-C14-1: the sum rule shown on two dependent days, with X + Y worked row by row
# (the original's general outcome-by-outcome proof was tried first and raised the mean sentence
# length above the original's, so validate.py failed it; this is the original's worked case instead)
illustration.body::Does the sum rule need the days to be independent?
illustration.body::Try a pair that is as tied together as possible.
illustration.body::Say that on day 2 this person's surplus is always 140 minus day 1's.
illustration.body::A big day is followed by a small one.
illustration.body::Call day 2's surplus Y.
illustration.body::Each value of X fixes Y.
illustration.body::Work out E[Y] from its own values and probabilities.
illustration.body::E[Y] is 70 too.
illustration.body::On every row, X plus Y is 140, so E[X + Y] is 140.
illustration.body::The sum rule gives the same.
illustration.body::The two days could hardly be more dependent, and the rule still holds.
