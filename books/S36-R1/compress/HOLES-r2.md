# S36-R1 compression pass: holes, batch r2 (C08 to C14)

Holes the cold read found that the full-length original does not fill, or that are errors or
contradictions rather than missing text. None can be closed by restoring; each goes to the fixer
and back through the audit. Gap ids are those in `COLD-READ-GAPS.md`. "As it stands" quotes the
final text (`<S>-final-prose.yml`) or, for exercises, `<S>-original.md` (exercises are not cut).

## C08

**H1 · C08-5 · C08 `definition.text` / `simplified_explanation`**
- As it stands: "The interview closes with thanks." (must_know[4] now also has "Then do come back
  to it: give them a name at the clinic to ask.")
- Missing: how to close: a closing question ("anything else?"), when the recorder goes off, what to
  do if the respondent is upset, and what to do with what is said after the recorder is off.
- To close: a short closing sequence in the Definition and one must-know on off-recorder talk
  (whether it may be used, and that it goes in field notes).

**H2 · C08-6 (and cross-cutting "no worked field notes") · C08 `simplified_explanation`; C09 Ex 2**
- As it stands: "Then, the same day, write field notes — what happened, where, how it felt, what
  surprised you."
- Missing: a worked set of field notes; C09 Ex 2 and C14 Ex 1 then ask the reader to write them.
- To close: one short model set of field notes for a made-up clinic interview (headings plus two or
  three lines each), in C08's illustration or C09's.

**H3 · C08-8 · C08 `must_know[0].point`**
- As it stands: "Running an interview well does not mean never steering. Britten's point is that
  the amount of steering should be right for the moment, not zero."
- Missing: a criterion for when to bring someone back to the guide, and how (what words).
- To close: a rule of thumb (for example, when a thread has stopped yielding new material or time
  for the remaining guide topics is running out) and a model return sentence built from the
  respondent's words.
