1. **gap** · The section says data rules on recordings are not covered (must_know[7] or nearby).
   Replace that line with a short teaching of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 as it
   bears on an interview recording: (a) the Act's own definitions (in force): personal data, Data
   Principal (the person), Data Fiduciary (who decides why and how data is processed), processing;
   an audio recording and a transcript that can identify someone carry personal data (say this as
   applying the definition — quote the definition). (b) **Commencement, the most important fact**:
   the definitions are in force from 13 November 2025; notice, consent, withdrawal, the duties in
   s.8, children's data and the exemptions including research (s.17(2)(b)) come into force
   "eighteen months from the date of publication" of G.S.R. 843(E) of 13 November 2025. Show the
   month count in one line (13 November 2025 + 18 months = 13 May 2027) as arithmetic, not as the
   notification's words. As of this book's date they are enacted, not in force. (c) The research
   exemption, when in force: the Act does not apply to processing "necessary for research" if the
   data is not used for a decision about that person and the processing follows the Second
   Schedule's standards — list them briefly (lawful; only for that purpose; only the data needed;
   reasonable accuracy; kept only as long as needed; security safeguards; an accountable person).
   (d) What the text leaves open, stated as open: "research" is not defined; nothing says whether a
   learner's practice interview counts; nothing ties the exemption to ethics-committee review. So
   the rule for the reader stays: the ethics committee (and the institution) decides; design the
   pilot as if the Act's duties applied, because ICMR 2017 already asks for most of the same (notice,
   voluntary consent, limited access, retention for the required period).
