## Gate

**Blocked: nine lines say no.** Six can be taken at intake without Harsh: DeJonckheere & Vaughn 2019, Pope, Ziebland & Mays 2000, Mays & Pope 2000, Green & Britten 1998, McMullin 2023 (HTML in PMC), and the ICMR 2017 PDF (text layer). Three are scanned 1995 *BMJ* articles in PMC with no HTML text: Britten, Kitzinger, and Pope & Mays. Intake should first try TinyFish on each PMC PDF link. If no text comes back, Harsh has to open the PDF in a browser, where the page images can be read, and supply it. Any passage taken from a page image must then be copied by hand and checked by a second reader, because the quote check cannot see it.

- **Fallbacks, if the scans cannot be read.** Pope & Mays 1995 can be dropped, because Green & Britten 1998 carries C01. Britten 1995 is shared with DeJonckheere & Vaughn 2019 on every concept it serves, so C03, C05, C08 and C09 could rest on the 2019 paper alone. Kitzinger 1995 is the only source for C04 (focus groups). Without it, C04 has no anchor and must wait or find another open paper at intake.
- **C07 is `institutional` and blocks without ICMR 2017 opened.** The drafter must quote what the guidelines say on review of research done as part of a course, and must not assert whether a learner's pilot interview needs committee review. The rule for the reader is to ask their own institutional ethics committee.
- **C11's source is uncertain.** It is `derivable`, so an unopened anchor only warns. Whether DeJonckheere & Vaughn say in so many words that the participant should do most of the talking is to be checked at intake. If they do not, cite the map's gate and Book 0 A4 and A5, and nothing else.
- **Copyright.** The BMJ articles are free to read, not openly licensed. Short quotations for audit are fine. The drafter must not reproduce boxes or tables.
