# S36-R1 holes from the compression pass, batch r1 (C01 to C07)

For the fixer. Each hole: section and field, the sentence as it stands in the final text, what is
wrong or missing, and what would close it. None of these can be closed by restoring the original.

**H-1 · C02 Ex 3 (and C13 Ex 2).** "Write the page: answer first." The one-page, answer-first brief
is taught nowhere. Close: a short worked example of the format (answer sentence, reason, what would
settle it) in C02 or a Book 0 pointer to where it is taught.

**H-2 · Sources without references (C04 definition.text, C02 must_know[3] original, C05
definition.text, C06 definition.text).** "(Kitzinger 1995)"; "DeJonckheere and Vaughn's guide";
Britten (C05 original). No title, journal or place anywhere. Close: full references in each
record's `sources`, and a first-mention gloss (e.g. "Kitzinger's 1995 BMJ paper on focus groups")
after verifying each.

**H-3 · C02 simplified_explanation.** "You are after the widest spread of reasons, not an average."
No way is given to decide which differences between people matter before the reasons are known
(the original defers purposive sampling to the next book). Close: one or two sentences on starting
from what might plausibly change the answer (distance, work, family, having kept coming), including
a case that might break the first guess.

**H-4 · Terms before their home section.** C03 Ex 2 and C04 Ex 1 use "I", "R2", "M" (explained in
C10); C04 must_know[0] "Read your transcript" and the figure's "turn" (C10, C11); C05 "probe"
(C08); C05 P4, P9 "topic guide" (C06). Close: a first-use gloss or pointer at each site.

**H-5 · C03 Ex 3.** "Why would a research interview need any new skill?" At C03 only the
prohibitions exist; probes and waiting arrive in C08. Close: a forward pointer to C08 in the text,
or move the exercise to C08.

**H-6 · Figures of material not shown (C04 figure; C05 figure).** C04: "Who each respondent's turn
answered, in the two made-up groups." C05: "The eight questions of the made-up first draft and of the
rewrite". Neither set of material appears. Close: show the two excerpts / the two question lists,
or recaption the figures so they do not refer to unseen material.

**H-7 · C04 simplified_explanation.** "In a focus group the moderator asks people to talk to each
other." Beyond the facilitator's opening explanation, running a group (opening, handling the loud
voice) is not shown. Close: a short worked moderator opening and one move for a dominant voice, if
the rung's outcomes need the reader to run one; else state that running groups is taught later.

**H-8 · C05 simplified_explanation, Check 1.** "Could the person answer with yes, no, a number, or
one of the options you gave them? Then it is a closed question." No ruling for "how much / how
often" questions answerable by a degree word or a number (P10 "How much does your weight worry
you?", "How often do you miss the evening walk?"). Close: one sentence ruling on degree and
frequency questions, consistent with the P2d, P8 and P10 answer keys.

**H-9 · C06 definition.text and simplified_explanation.** "A guide is tried out before the study,
in a pilot interview." / "see which questions fall flat". "Pilot" is defined only in C14; "fall
flat" has no test anywhere (C14 Ex 1 depends on it). Close: gloss "pilot" at first use; give a
test for "fell flat" (e.g. answered yes/no, answered as a different question, or respondent's own
words never came).

**H-10 · C06 simplified_explanation.** "Prompts under each main question. Short notes of the things
you hope the answer covers." No example prompt, and no word on a prompt that names a topic the
respondent has not raised versus C08's rule that a probe bringing in a new word leads. Close: one
example prompt, and a sentence reconciling prompts with C08.

**H-11 · C06 (Ex 1).** No worked topic guide (warm-up, main questions, prompts, closing, time plan)
exists in the original; the clinic guide's five main questions are never shown. Close: show the
clinic guide whose time plan the figure draws.

**H-12 · C06 figure.** "Most of the time goes to the three main questions that carry the study
question." Which three, and why the others do not, is not said. Close: name them (mains 2 to 4) and
what mains 1 and 5 are for.

**H-13 · C06 simplified_explanation.** "Easy and factual first, personal later." Whether closed
demographic questions (age, household, income) belong in the guide, and where; how this squares
with C08's open first question. Close: one sentence.

**H-14 · C06 must_know[2].** "Write each main question from a part of your study question" is
asserted, never demonstrated. Close: split the example study question into parts and show one main
question from each.

**H-15 · C07 definition.text.** "providing relevant information to potential participants" — the
restored text now names freedom to withdraw and the limits of confidentiality, but not purpose, who
the researcher is, duration, recording, what happens to the words, or contact. No worked consent
script exists. Close: list the ICMR Box 5.1 elements relevant here, after checking them; or a
worked spoken consent.

**H-16 · C07 Ex 2.** "so it's minimal risk under ICMR." "Minimal risk" is never defined, and the
Definition names review types, not risk categories. Close: define ICMR's risk categories in one or
two sentences, from Table 4.2 / section 4.8, after checking them.

**H-17 · C07 simplified_explanation.** "You send the plan, and the committee decides." Which
committee (a learner may have no institution), what the plan contains, what to do with the reply.
Close: a short "what to send" list and where a learner without an institution goes.

**H-18 · C07 must_know[6] (with C08 and C09).** "Tell people the limits of confidentiality, not a
promise of perfect secrecy." The concrete limits are not stated; C08's duty to disclose danger to
life is one, and C09's "Promise, truthfully, that nothing said will reach the clinic" contradicts
both. Close: state the limits here (including disclosure to save life) and make C09's promise
consistent with them. This is an error, not only a gap.

**H-19 · C07 must_know[1].** "plan where the recording lives and who hears it" — storage is named,
not taught: device, access, retention, deletion. Close: a few concrete rules, sourced.

**H-20 · C07 Ex 3.** Using recordings in a presentation: consent for secondary use and
identifiability of a voice are not addressed; the restored masking rule (section 9.2.12) is the
nearest. Close: one sentence that playing recordings to others needs it in the consent and the EC's
approval, after checking the guidelines.

**H-21 · Respondent labels reused (C03, C04, C05, and on through C14).** R2 and others are
different people in different files while C01 presents the clinic as one running setting. Close:
say once (C01 or C03) that each excerpt is a separate made-up interview and labels restart, or give
running labels unique identities.

# S36-R1 compression pass: holes, batch r2 (C08 to C14)

Holes the cold read found that the full-length original does not fill, or that are errors or
contradictions rather than missing text. None can be closed by restoring; each goes to the fixer
and back through the audit. Gap ids are those in `COLD-READ-GAPS.md`. "As it stands" quotes the
final text (`<S>-final-prose.yml`) or, for exercises, `<S>-original.md` (exercises are not cut).

## C08

**H1 · C08-5 · C08 `definition.text` / `simplified_explanation`**
- As it stands: "The interview closes with thanks." (must_know[4] now also has "Then do come back
  to it: give them a name at the clinic to ask.")
- Missing: how to close: a closing question ("anything else?"), when the recorder goes off, what to
  do if the respondent is upset, and what to do with what is said after the recorder is off.
- To close: a short closing sequence in the Definition and one must-know on off-recorder talk
  (whether it may be used, and that it goes in field notes).

**H2 · C08-6 (and cross-cutting "no worked field notes") · C08 `simplified_explanation`; C09 Ex 2**
- As it stands: "Then, the same day, write field notes — what happened, where, how it felt, what
  surprised you."
- Missing: a worked set of field notes; C09 Ex 2 and C14 Ex 1 then ask the reader to write them.
- To close: one short model set of field notes for a made-up clinic interview (headings plus two or
  three lines each), in C08's illustration or C09's.

**H3 · C08-8 · C08 `must_know[0].point`**
- As it stands: "Running an interview well does not mean never steering. Britten's point is that
  the amount of steering should be right for the moment, not zero."
- Missing: a criterion for when to bring someone back to the guide, and how (what words).
- To close: a rule of thumb (for example, when a thread has stopped yielding new material or time
  for the remaining guide topics is running out) and a model return sentence built from the
  respondent's words.

## C09

**H4 · C09-4 (with C08-7, C07) · C09 `simplified_explanation`; also must_know[3]** · **serious**
- As it stands: "Promise, truthfully, that nothing said will reach the clinic." and must_know[3]
  "Say that nothing the person says will change their care."
- Wrong: C07 says "Tell people the limits of confidentiality, not a promise of perfect secrecy";
  C08 must_know[6] now says "tell people at consent that confidentiality has limits" and describes
  a duty to disclose danger to life. The C09 promise can be untrue.
- To close: rewrite the C09 promise to match C07/C08 (for example: nothing said will be passed to
  the clinic in a way that names you, except where a life is in danger), and give the exact
  sentence once, in C07's consent script, with C09 pointing to it.

**H5 · C09-3, C10-2, C12-3, C14-6 · C09 must_know[5], [6]; C10 illustration; C12
analogy_breaks_when; C14 definition**
- As it stands: "which is second-rung work"; "Your build target for this rung is five pilot
  interviews."; "S36-R2 turns first labels into a set of codes ..."; "The build for this rung is
  five pilot interviews."
- Missing: "rung", "build (target)" and "S36-R2" are course-internal words never glossed for the
  reader (the same course-wide hole as S01-R1 GL-2).
- To close: gloss once, at first use in the book (C01 or the book's front matter): a rung is one
  level of this subject; S36-R2 is the next level; the build is the thing the reader produces to
  finish the rung.
