# S36-R1 · Qualitative and mixed methods, rung 1 · concept inventory

Task 1 of `PIPELINE.md`, by the method in the specification §5, against frozen map `fc19c8bc-a216-4131-99fb-ebe3f19cec4a`; `python check/amendments.py --rung S36-R1` prints no amendments for S36-R1 or S36-R2, so none is marked `verify_at_intake`. Serves: O1–O3 rung-1 outcomes, S1–S2 skills, B build target (five pilot interviews conducted, transcribed and read), G gate (the respondent speaks more than you do); P1–P4 rung-2 concepts, R1–R3 rung-2 skills, B2 the rung-2 build target, only as far as rung 2 presupposes them. Book 0 teaches nothing qualitative, so nothing here re-teaches it; what it lends is the sample, the random sample and the convenience sample (D6 `C29`), the population against the person (D5 `C28`), bias as error that points one way (D7 `C30`), premise, inference and conclusion (F4 `C42`), checking a reference against its passage (F3 `C41`), percentages and ratios (A4 `C04`, A5 `C05`), and how government and its bodies make rules (F5 `C43`). S01-R1 and S02-R1 are not needed. Routed to S36-R2 and not taught here: purposive and theoretical sampling and saturation (P1), thematic and framework analysis and building a coding framework (P2, R1), interviewer effects in hierarchical Indian settings (P3), COREQ and reporting (P4, R3). Rung 1 carries only the doorstep of each: that a qualitative sample is chosen, not drawn (C02); that the interviewer shapes the answer (C09); what a transcript is and how it was made (C10); what a code is, at the level of labelling a passage while reading (C12); a quotation's attribution and context (C13). Mixed-methods designs are S36-R3. Quantitative is decided by §7a: a concept is quantitative when the reader must carry out a technique whose result can be checked. Two qualify. C05 (open, closed and leading questions) owes a drill set of classifying and rewriting questions: level 1–3, label a bare question open, closed or leading; 4–6, the same on questions from an Indian obesity topic guide, with the rewrite; 7–8, a worked rewrite that is still leading (a presupposition kept, a "why" that accuses) for the reader to break; 9–10, a line from a draft protocol ("we will ask patients whether they find the diet hard") to turn into a guide question and say what the answer still cannot show. About 8–10 problems. C11 (talk share) owes a drill set of counting and arithmetic: word counts to a share, share across five interviews, the diagnostic of counting turns instead of words, and the claim "I barely spoke" tested against a transcript. About 6–8 problems. Everything else is done with people and is demonstrated by exercises and the build, not drilled. Type: d derivable, e empirical, i institutional.
