1. **error** (from compression hole H4, with H-18) · `simplified_explanation` "Promise, truthfully,
   that nothing said will reach the clinic", and illustration 1 "nothing you say will go back to
   them", which is shown as the good version. This is not true. C07 `must_know[6]` and ICMR 2.3.2
   require telling people confidentiality "may not be possible" in every circumstance. C08
   `must_know[6]` and ICMR 9.2.8 describe a duty to disclose to save life. ICMR 9.2.12 has findings
   shared "in aggregated form" with community leaders and gatekeepers, so findings do reach the clinic.
   Fix: "Promise, truthfully, that the clinic will not know who said what,
   and say the limits you gave at consent"; in illustration 1: "I do not work at the clinic, and
   the clinic will not know who said what." (C09 illustration 2 and exercise 2 already use that
   wording.) **recurring** (SELFCHECK 5/7, cross-section contradiction).
    Fixer: simplified_explanation now "Promise, truthfully, that the clinic will not know who said what, and say the limits you gave at consent (`S36-R1-C07`)"; illustration 1 quote now "...and the clinic will not know who said what." Exercise 3 aligned the same way.
    Verifier: closed
2. **error** · `definition.text` "They describe its direction: towards the answer the respondent
   thinks the interviewer wants. It is bias, and more interviews by the same interviewer in the same
   setting do not shrink it." Britten gives a direction only for the doctor case ("may wish to
   please the doctor"); her general case ("class, race, sex, and social distance") and Mays and
   Pope's ("age, sex, social class, and professional status") give none. The section's own
   `analogy_breaks_when` says a stranger may hear "a harsher story", which is another direction.
   The last sentence is the book's inference from Book 0 (B0-R0-C30), not something the sources
   say. Fix: "Where the interviewer is a doctor or clinic staff, Britten names the direction:
   towards what the respondent thinks the doctor wants. Whatever the direction, it is the same in
   every interview one person runs in one setting. By Book 0's reasoning on systematic error, that
   makes it bias, and more such interviews do not shrink it." **recurring** (stronger than source,
   SELFCHECK 4).
    Fixer: definition's last paragraph replaced with the auditor's wording (direction named only for the doctor/clinic case; bias by Book 0's reasoning).
    Verifier: closed
