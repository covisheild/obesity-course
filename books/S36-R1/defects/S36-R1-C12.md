1. **`must_know[5]` (boundary) and `illustration.analogy_breaks_when` ¶2**
   - **Claim:** "Until someone else can apply your codes and reach the same passages, do not present
     them as the study's results." / "S36-R2 turns first labels into a set of codes that a second
     person can apply and reach the same passages."
   - **Source:** no reference backs this. The held source says the opposite is contested: Pope,
     Ziebland and Mays 2000, "Inter-rater reliability": "Some researchers have found that the use of
     more than one analyst can improve the consistency or reliability of analyses. However, the
     appropriateness of the concept of inter-rater reliability in qualitative research is
     contested." The course map's R2 gate (second coder) is a course decision, not a rule of the
     method.
   - **Fix:** soften to "Many researchers check first codes by having a second person apply them;
     whether that is required is disputed. Until your codes have been checked in some way, present
     them as your working notes." Add the Pope reference with the quote above.
   - **Tag:** error. **Recurring** (claim stronger than its source; SELFCHECK 4).
   Fixer: must_know[5] softened to the suggested wording; analogy ¶2 now says only that rung 2 takes up checking with a second person; two Pope "Inter-rater reliability" quotes added to definition.references and must_know[5].refs.
