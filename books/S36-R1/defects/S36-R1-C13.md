# Audit defects · S36-R1-C13 · The quotation as data

Task 3 audit, 24 September 2026 (batch a5). I searched every quote in its mapped file
(`dejonckheere_vaughn_2019.txt`, `mays_pope_2000.txt`, `pope_ziebland_mays_2000.txt`,
`blackstone_2012.txt`, including Blackstone's Table 9.1 paragraph at line 691): all seven are there
word for word. The Pope "decontextualisation" sentence is about software, and its note says it is
cited only for the named problem; acceptable. No quote states a number and none needs one. No
`quote` field changed. Kinds are right. The four acts match Book 0 C41's retrieval answer (find,
confirm it is the one named, find the passage, read whether it supports the claim). No arithmetic,
no practice set, no figure (`figure_note`). No dates or cut-points. Build: blocking 0, no warnings
on this record.

1. **`definition.text` ¶3** · "Choosing only the quotations that fit the author's view, or cutting
   a quotation so that it fits, is cherry-picking. It fails the last of those checks."
   - **What the text itself shows:** true of *cutting* a quotation (Exercise 1). Not true of
     *choosing only* the ones that fit: each chosen quotation can pass all four acts, as R1, R3 and
     R5 would in illustration 2. Selection is caught by reading across the transcripts, which is
     what illustration 2 does and what Mays and Pope's "Attention to negative cases" and "Fair
     dealing" quotes are about. As written, a reader will believe the four acts catch selection.
   - **Fix:** "Cutting a quotation so that it fits fails the last of those checks. Choosing only
     the quotations that fit passes all four; you catch it only by reading the other transcripts
     for what runs against the claim."
   - **Tag:** error. **Recurring** (SELFCHECK 5: the record teaches a rule its own example breaks).

2. **Exercise 1 `answer`** · "Find it: ... They are not there." then "Find the place: this is where
   the first failure shows."
   - The answer already says at act one that the words are not in the transcript, then says the
     first failure shows at act three. The two contradict, and the reader is taught the acts in the
     wrong order of failure.
   - **Fix:** "Find it: search for the quoted words. They are not there, which is the first
     failure. The nearest line is ..." and at act three: "Find the place: the nearest line is her
     third sentence. 'He looked' became 'They look' ..."
   - **Tag:** error.
