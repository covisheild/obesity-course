## Decisions taken in this book (the conductor, 24 Sep 2026)

- **Reference kind.** Drafters had labelled journal methods papers `textbook` to pass the kind
  rule. Reverted to `primary`, and an open textbook (`blackstone_2012`) was taken in instead.
- **Illustrations are made up and say so.** One running setting: a district hospital NCD clinic in
  Chhattisgarh whose patients with obesity stop coming back.
- Book-wide rules for the fixers (`FIX-BRIEF.md`): confidentiality with limits, never absolute;
  signed consent form carries the name and is kept apart from recording and transcript; "code" vs
  "label"; "how often / how many / how long" questions are closed; the gate is under 50 per cent of
  words, counted in words.
- `check/**` was not touched (frozen mid-flight under `PARALLEL.md`), so no diagram kind was added
  to the figure tool; the eight `figure_note`s name the diagrams a later tool could draw.
