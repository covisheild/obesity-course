Descending order

Description:

     Transform a vector into a format that will be sorted in descending
     order. This is useful within 'arrange()'.

Usage:

     desc(x)
     
Arguments:

       x: vector to transform

Examples:

     desc(1:10)
     desc(factor(letters))
     
     first_day <- seq(as.Date("1910/1/1"), as.Date("1920/1/1"), "years")
     desc(first_day)
     
     starwars %>% arrange(desc(mass))
