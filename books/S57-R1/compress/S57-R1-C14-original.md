# S57-R1-C14 · Where teaching sits in Indian medical education

**Definition.** Undergraduate medical education in India is governed by the Graduate Medical Education
Regulations, 2023 (GMER 2023). The National Medical Commission (NMC) notified them in the
Gazette on 2 June 2023. They direct NMC's undergraduate board to publish the model curriculum
on the NMC website from time to time. A separate circular of 12 June 2023, issuing guidelines
under these regulations, was withdrawn on 23 June 2023; the regulations were not.

The curriculum in force is NMC's Competency Based Medical Education (CBME) Curriculum 2024,
enclosed with a letter of 12 September 2024. It calls itself guidelines, it was issued by
letter, and it places itself under GMER 2023. The competency-based curriculum itself began
with the MBBS admission batch of 2019.

The CBME Curriculum 2024 names seven roles for the Indian Medical Graduate (IMG): clinician;
leader and member of the health care team and system; communicator; lifelong learner;
professional; critical thinker; and researcher. The 2019 regulation named the first five.

It requires a Foundation Course of two weeks, 80 teaching hours, at the start of Phase I. It
requires a longitudinal module on Attitude, Ethics and Communication (AETCOM) across the
course, with at least 75% attendance for eligibility to sit the university examinations. It
limits large group teaching to one third of a subject's allotted hours. It pairs each mentor
with three mentees, the mentors drawn from Assistant Professor to Professor. Mentees are
allotted during the Foundation Course and stay with their mentor until they complete the
compulsory rotating medical internship (CRMI).

GMER 2023 requires teaching faculty to be trained in group dynamics, team building,
small-group teaching and guiding self-directed learning.

The Medical Institutions (Qualifications of Faculty) Regulations, 2025 were notified on 30
June 2025. They superseded the Teachers Eligibility Qualifications in Medical Institutions
Regulations, 2022. Their Table E covers faculty in broad specialties, the MD and MS subjects.
There a Professor or an Associate Professor "shall be required to undergo" the Basic Course
in Medical Education. The proviso is that the specialty is covered under undergraduate
training. Regulation 14 exempts three groups: faculty in super specialties, faculty in broad
specialties outside the undergraduate curriculum, and, subject to a proviso, faculty in
institutes of national importance.

**In plain terms.** You have met one paper from the National Medical Commission (NMC) already. The section on
tests that match the objective used its levels K, KH, SH and P. This section sets that paper
beside the others that shape your teaching.

Teaching in an Indian medical college is not left to each teacher's taste. It is shaped by
papers NMC has issued, and you will be asked about them.

At the top sits a regulation, the Graduate Medical Education Regulations, 2023 (GMER 2023). It
was notified in the Gazette, so it binds. It is short. It says the curriculum will be published
on the NMC website, and it says teachers must be trained to teach small groups.

Under it sits the detail, in a guideline — a document NMC's undergraduate board sent out by
letter and put on its website. The one in force is the Competency Based Medical Education
(CBME) Curriculum 2024. "Competency based" means the course is built from what a graduate must
be able to do, not from a list of topics to cover.

That guideline names the doctor the course is meant to produce: the Indian Medical Graduate, or
IMG. It gives the IMG seven roles. Two of them are the reason this book exists: leader and member
of the health care team, and lifelong learner.

It also fixes things you will run into as a teacher. First-year students start with a two-week
Foundation Course. A module on attitude, ethics and communication runs through all the years.
Lectures to a large group may take at most a third of a subject's hours. Each mentor gets three
students to look after.

A third paper decides what you need to hold as a teacher. It is the faculty regulations of 2025.
They tie a course, the Basic Course in Medical Education, to the Associate Professor and
Professor posts in most MD and MS subjects. Community Medicine is one of them.

**Must know points for you.**

- "The Indian Medical Graduate has five roles" is out of date. The CBME Curriculum 2024 lists seven: critical thinker and researcher were added to the 2019 five. The curriculum manual inside the same document still prints five, so quote the guidelines' list, not the manual's copy of it.
- Large group teaching may take at most one third of a subject's allotted hours under the CBME Curriculum 2024. When a department asks you to lecture, count its large-group hours first. A timetable over that third is out of line with the curriculum, however good the lectures are.
- Mentoring is in the curriculum, not an optional extra. Each mentor gets three students during the Foundation Course and keeps them until they finish internship. Mentors are drawn from Assistant Professor upwards, so expect to be one from your first post as Assistant Professor.
- The Basic Course in Medical Education is tied to posts, not to teaching as such. Table E of the 2025 faculty regulations names it for Professors and Associate Professors. It does so in MD and MS subjects covered under undergraduate training. Its Assistant Professor row does not name it. Say that, not "every teacher must do it".
- Know which kind of paper you are quoting. GMER 2023 is a regulation, notified in the Gazette. The CBME Curriculum 2024 is a guideline sent by letter under it. A circular of 12 June 2023 that issued guidelines under GMER 2023 was withdrawn eleven days later; the regulations themselves were not. So a colleague waving "the 2023 guidelines" may be waving a withdrawn circular. Ask for its date.
- Two papers in force can disagree. GMER 2023 bars a student from continuing after nine years from admission. The CBME Curriculum 2024 says a learner may not graduate later than ten years from joining, internship included. When a student's case turns on the limit, name both papers and their words. Do not pick one yourself.
- These instruments tell you what a college must provide and what a teacher must hold. They say nothing about whether any session works. A college can meet every hour in them and its students can still learn little. Whether they learned is settled only by a test that matches the objective.
