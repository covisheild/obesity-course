Attention is hereby drawn to CBME Guideline, 2024 published on
12.09.2024 in the NMC Website.
2.
 
In light of the observations made by the Hon’ble Court of Madras, the
Corrigendum/ addendum in above CBME Guidelines, 2024 has been carried
out in due 
consultation with the experts in the field of medical education, 
which
is attached herewith for the perusal of the stakeholders.
3.
 
The various regulations are applicable as and when they are published and
NMC circulates it to all the colleges. The contents of the MBBS curriculum are
a guide to the faculty for teaching, learning and for carrying out assessment,
hence each guideline cannot be added verbatim in curriculum. Curriculum
deﬁnes competencies, suggests teaching learning and assessment methods.
Colleges and teachers are welcome to teach all regulations so that the
students become competent.
 
4.
 
It will be appreciated if the persons writing in media need to thoroughly go
through the relevant sections of the referred guidelines published by
respective boards of NMC and subsequently address the same to the NMC to
implement any relevant part as applicable. This will help to improve curriculum
and lead to redressal on priority.
