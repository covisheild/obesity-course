1. **error** · `must_know[1].point`
   - Claim: "In Deslauriers's experiment, a chance process decided who was taught actively. Those
     students scored almost half a standard deviation higher on the end-of-class test. They rated
     their learning more than half a standard deviation lower."
   - Source: "Each student experienced both types of instruction in a crossover study design"
     (Materials and Methods, control 5). Table 2: group A was active for statics and passive for
     fluids, and group B the reverse. Chance decided the order, not who was taught actively. The
     0.46 and −0.56 are within-student contrasts (active against passive classes), from a regression
     with student covariates. "Those students" makes it read as a between-group result.
   - Fix: "In Deslauriers's experiment, every student had one class taught actively and one taught
     by lecture, in an order decided by chance. In their active class they scored almost half a
     standard deviation higher on the end-of-class test, and rated their learning more than half a
     standard deviation lower."
   - Recurring (SELFCHECK 4a: a finding stated beyond what was measured, here the design).

2. **error** · `definition.text`, paragraph 3, with `definition.references[0..2]`
   - Claim: "In Roediger and Karpicke's first experiment (2006), each student read two short prose
     passages. They then read one passage again and took a recall test on the other."

     figure and retrieval items all use Experiment 2. The three references cited for it quote the

   - Fix: delete the paragraph and its three references from C01, and let C03 introduce Experiment
     1 (it already does). If it stays, quote the Design sentence instead of the results.
   - Recurring (SELFCHECK 1: quotes that do not carry the sentence they are cited for).

3. **gap** · `simplified_explanation`, paragraph 3 (from compression hole H-1)
   - Claim: "You find out about learning by testing again, after a gap, on something the learners
     have not just seen."
   - "Something the learners have not just seen" can be read as different material, which
     contradicts must-know point 2 ("the same objective"). "After a gap" also breaks the book's
     notation (DRAFT-BRIEF "Notation"): a gap is the time between two study sessions (C04), and the
     time from study to test is the retention interval, which C01 has just defined.
   - Fix: "You find out about learning by testing again after a delay, a week or more, with new
     questions on the same objective."
   - Recurring (SELFCHECK 7: one term for one thing).
