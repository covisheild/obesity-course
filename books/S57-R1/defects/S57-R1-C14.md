## Defects

1. **definition.text, paragraph 1.** Claim: "The National Medical Commission (NMC) notified them in
   the Gazette on 2 June 2023." The statute and the power are missing, and F5's reading needs both.
   Source (GMER 2023 heading): "In exercise of overall powers conferred by the National Medical
   Commission Act, 2019 vis-à-vis medical education in general and specifically by sub-section (1)
   of section 24; and sub-section (h), ... of sub-section (2) of section 57 of the said Act". Fix:
   add "NMC made them under the National Medical Commission Act, 2019, sections 24(1) and 57(2)",
   with that quote. **gap** (from compression hole 1, H19)

2. **simplified_explanation and definition.text, paragraph 2.** The guideline's legal standing is
   never stated. Source: GMER 2023 regulation 21 says "Every student admitted in accordance with
   these Regulations, shall undergo training as per the guidelines published by the UGMEB of NMC and
   displayed on the NMC website from time to time". Regulation 20 makes the model curriculum "the
   base for the development of a detailed medical curriculum by the concerned Universities". NMC's
   own clarification of October 2024 (held in the same file) adds that "The contents of the MBBS
   curriculum are a guide to the faculty for teaching, learning and for carrying out assessment".
   Fix: one sentence: "GMER 2023 requires every student to be trained as the board's published
   guidelines say, so the guideline binds through the regulation", quoting regulation 21. Also
   replace "it places itself under GMER 2023". The preamble quote only says GMER 2023 "was placed
   last year"; it does not place the guideline under it. **gap** (from compression hole 2, H20)

3. **definition.text, paragraph 4, and simplified_explanation.** "Large group" is never defined. The
   plain version narrows the rule: "Lectures to a large group may take at most a third". Source (the
   curriculum's "Explanation of terms"): "LGT (LGT) Any instructional large group method including
   interactive lec ture". The curriculum gives no group size. Fix: quote the definition; say the
   curriculum sets no size; write "Teaching to a large group, by any method, may take at most a
   third". **gap** (the narrowing is an **error**) (from compression hole 3, H21)

4. **definition.text, paragraph 4.** Claim: "It limits large group teaching to one third ..." Since
   compression, "It" follows "The 2019 regulation named the first five." and reads as the 2019
   regulation. Fix: "The CBME Curriculum 2024 limits large group teaching ...". **error**,
   **recurring** (pronoun whose referent was cut)

5. **exercises[2] (teaching).** The prompt asks "Why does the CBME Curriculum 2024 cap large group
   teaching at one third". The curriculum gives no reason; checked in the held p. 27, and the answer
   itself says it cites no study. Fix: reword the prompt: "What does the cap force a department to
   do, what argument from this book supports it, and what does the cap not guarantee?" **gap**
   (from compression hole 4, H22)

6. **definition.text, paragraph 3.** Claim: "The 2019 regulation named the first five." The
   regulation is not named. Source (Mahajan, held): "the 'Regulations on Graduate Medical Education
   (Amendment), 2019,' which were incorporated as Part II of the 'Regulations on Graduate Medical
   Education, 1997'". Fix: name it; the Mahajan quote already in the record can carry it. **gap**
   (from compression hole 5, H23)

7. **definition.text, paragraph 5.** "the MD and MS subjects": MS is never expanded, and **MD
   collides with the book's own notation**. From section 2 on, MD means the mean difference. Fix:
   "the Doctor of Medicine (MD) and Master of Surgery (MS) subjects. This MD is the degree, not the
   mean difference of section 2." Or write the degree names out and avoid "MD" here. **gap**,
   **recurring** (acronym before expansion; notation drift)

8. **must_know[4].** The withdrawal of 12 June and 23 June 2023 is stated in the book's own voice.
   The withdrawal circular is not held, and the record's own note says it rests on Mahajan and Gupta.
   The point also misses the other "2023 guidelines": the CBME Guideline of 1 August 2023. The held
   CBME file's header says the 2024 guidelines replaced it. It was superseded, not withdrawn. Fix:
   "Mahajan and Gupta (2024) report that ... was withdrawn eleven days later". Add: "A CBME guideline
   of 1 August 2023 was later replaced by the 2024 curriculum. Ask for the date and check which one
   it is." **gap**

9. **Figure `img-roles`, caption.** Claim: the 2024 guidelines "name seven, adding critical thinker
   and researcher". Source (Mahajan): "The CBME Guidelines 2023 have expanded ... increasing the roles
   from five to seven". The two roles were added in 2023, not by the 2024 document. Fix: "name seven,
   including critical thinker and researcher, which were added after 2019". **error**

10. **illustrations[1].body.** Claim: "The table does not say whether you need the teaching course
    before the promotion or may take it after." That is incomplete. Table E row 2, Note 2 (held)
    says a directly appointed Associate Professor "shall be required to undergo Basic Course in
    Medical Education and Basic Course in Biomedical Research within two years of the
    appointment". FAQ Q5 (held) repeats it. So the table does set a time, after appointment, for
    that one route, and is silent for the others. Also, only FAQ Q5 of the October 2025 FAQs is
    held; the others may answer the question. Fix: add the Note 2 sentence. Then: "For the ordinary
    route it says nothing. Read NMC's FAQs of 28 October 2025 before you write to NMC." **gap**
    (the FAQs are a currency item)

11. **retrieval_items[2].** It asks for the Foundation Course's length and hours ("Two weeks ... 80
    teaching hours"). No reader-facing field teaches either number. Fix: add one sentence to the
    definition, with the two quotes already held (references[13] and [14]), or drop the item.
    **gap**, **recurring** (a retrieval item on untaught content)

12. **simplified_explanation.** Claim: "most MD and MS subjects". No held source counts the
    exempted subjects. Regulation 14 exempts "broad specialty subjects not included in the
    undergraduate curriculum", with no number. Fix: "in MD and MS subjects that are taught to
    undergraduates". **style**

13. **provenance.outcome_refs: S57-R1-A01.** A01 (leading is a learnable craft; lineage, team and
