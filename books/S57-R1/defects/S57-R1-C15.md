# Audit defects · S57-R1-C15 · Leading people is a craft of its own

Auditor: Task 3 (batch a4), 25 Sep 2026. Field indices are 0-based.

**Quotes.** All 23 quotes (16 `definition.references`, 7 illustration `numbers`) were found verbatim
in `frich_2015_physician_leadership.txt`, `stoller_2020_leadership.txt`,
`kotter_1990_what_leaders_do.txt` (ligatures kept) and `nmc_cbme_2024.txt`. Each number quote
states its number: Table 2 "Post | 22 (49)", "Pre/post | 23 (51)" and "Comparison group | 4 (9)";
"45 studies"; "3 new mentees". The 41 and the 15 carry `derived`. None was edited.

**Design of the cited work.** Frich 2015 is a systematic review of 45 peer-reviewed studies, found in
Ovid MEDLINE up to November 2013. Its limitations say the heterogeneity was "precluding a
quantitative synthesis": no pooled effect. Most assessments came "immediately after the program".
Kotter 1990 is an essay from observation of US corporations, with no study and no reference list.
Stoller 2020 is a single-author narrative review.

**Arithmetic.** Recomputed in Python: 22 + 23 = 45; 45 − 4 = 41; 3 × 5 = 15; the mentee points 0, 3,
6, 9, 12, 15 lie on y = 3x. All correct.

**Figures.** `study-designs`: the bars 22, 23 and 4 match Table 2, and both checks hold.
`mentees`: the rate of three a year is the held CBME text ("Each year when 3 new mentees are added").
The five years are labelled as the example's in the caption and the analogy. The conductor's note is
closed. (Minor: the dashed line implies a continuous flow, where mentees arrive in steps of three
each Foundation Course. Acceptable.)

**Amendments.** `S57-R1-A01` is served: learnable, not the same as the most competent person, and
the three things to build with a test to tell them apart. But "learnable, like teaching" is stated
further than Kotter goes (item 1). `S57-R1-O1` is served. Bridge `S57-R2-P05` (coalitions: professional
bodies and civil society) and `S57-R2-R3` (join one of each): the two terms are defined, but the
advice has no reason (item 7).

## Defects

1. **simplified_explanation, paragraph 2.** Claim: "Those who write about it, such as Kotter, say no.
   It can be learned, like teaching." Source: Kotter says leadership "is not the province of a chosen
   few". He also says companies "actively seek out people with leadership potential and expose them
   to career experiences designed to develop that potential", and "not everyone can be good at both
   leading and managing. Some people have the capacity to become excellent managers but not strong
   leaders." So Kotter's view is potential, spotted and then developed by experience. It is not "it
   can be learned". "Those who write about it" generalises one author to all. Fix: "Kotter says no:
   it is not the province of a chosen few. Companies find people with the potential and develop it
   through early challenge. He adds that not everyone becomes a strong leader." **error**,
   **recurring** (stated beyond the source)
   Fixer: confirmed in the held Kotter text ("actively seek out people with leadership potential ...", "Some people have the capacity to become excellent managers but not strong leaders"). Paragraph 2 now reads as the fix gives; two Kotter references added quoting those passages.
