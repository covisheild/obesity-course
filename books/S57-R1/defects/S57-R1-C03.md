2. **error** · `simplified_explanation`, paragraph 3; `illustrations[2].body`, "Each choice has a
   reason in the evidence"
   - Claim: "Three things make the method work in a classroom. Every learner answers, alone,
     without notes." Also: "Alone: Agarwal's review excluded pairs, because in a pair one student may
     do the remembering while the other watches."
   - "Alone" and "closed-book" were Agarwal's **inclusion criteria**. They define what the review
     counted as classroom retrieval. The review never tested whether they make the method work. The
     one pair-versus-individual study the source describes found the opposite: "students in the
     collaborative retrieval group outperformed students who had engaged in retrieval practice
     individually" (Sennhenn-Kirchner 2016). It was excluded because engagement "could not be
     guaranteed", not because pairs did worse. "Low stakes" rests on one study (Hinze and Rapp) as
     Weinstein reports it. Only feedback is supported by a moderator finding (items 3, 5).
   - Fix: "Three conditions define the classroom evidence below: every learner answers alone and
     without notes, the score counts for little, and the answer follows. Feedback is the one with
     the clearest evidence." In illustration 3: "Alone: this is what the evidence covers. The review
     left out pair work because it could not tell who had retrieved. The one pair study it describes
     did better than individuals, so 'alone' is the safe choice, not a proven one."
   - Recurring (SELFCHECK 4: a conclusion the source does not license).
   - Fixer: simplified explanation now says "use three conditions", that alone/no notes "is how the classroom studies below were run", and "feedback has the clearest evidence" (not "make the method work": low stakes was not an Agarwal criterion either, and 10 experiments had no feedback). Illustration 3's "Alone" reason rewritten per the fix, with a new reference quoting the Sennhenn-Kirchner result; retrieval item 2 now asks what "this section asks you to build in".
