  this correctly.

  calls it "a single study the reviewers say needs repeating". The review says the same.

  The questionnaire came at the end of Phase 1, and the source says the ratings did not differ

  fair. SSSS predicted better recall than both other groups; SSST and STTT "did not differ

6. **error** · `must_know[5].point`, with `definition.references[15]` (from compression hole 14,
   the C05 half; confirmed).
   - Claim: "No experiment has tested rereading's effect on learning in a real course".

     educational contexts". The present tense turns a 2013 statement into a current one. Section 3

     is what Dunlosky meant, but a reader cannot tell the two apart as the point stands.
   - Fix: "By 2013 no experiment had tested rereading against doing nothing in a real course. Later
     classroom experiments did set it against retrieval practice, and retrieval practice won
     (section 3)." Cite `agarwal_2021_retrieval_practice` with the k = 19 quote. Make the C03
     caption's "3 favoured the comparison" name the comparison (C03's auditor has hole 14's other
     half).
   - **Recurring** (SELFCHECK 4: a conclusion the source does not license. Present tense on a
     dated finding).
   - Fixer: now "By 2013 no experiment had tested rereading's effect on learning in a real course. Later classroom experiments often set rereading against retrieval practice (`S57-R1-C03`)." Added an `agarwal_2021_retrieval_practice` reference with the k = 19 quote. Did not write "retrieval practice won": the held text's k = 19 sentence gives the comparison condition, not a pooled result against rereading, so that clause was left out (decision 2). Kept Dunlosky's own words ("its impact on learning") rather than "against doing nothing". C03's caption belongs to C03's fixer.
