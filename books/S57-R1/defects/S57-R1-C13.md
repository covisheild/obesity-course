## Defects

1. **Figure `levels-measured`, spec and alt; illustrations[1] table, row "4".** Claim: "4 · system
   results, either kind · 6"; alt "Level 4, system results: 6", while the definition splits Levels 2,
   3 and 4 into A and B. Source: Table 2 reports Level 4 split: "System results/performance
   (subjective) (Level 4A) | 1 (2)" and "System results/performance (objective) (Level 4B) | 5 (11)";
   1 + 5 = 6 matches "reported in six articles". Fix: split the row and the bar into 4A = 1 and
   4B = 5, with those two Table 2 rows as `numbers` quotes, and add `check: "1 + 5 = 6"`. **gap**
   (from compression hole 1, H16)

2. **must_know[2] and retrieval_items[2].** Claim: "This book's gate asks that ...". "Gate" is defined
   nowhere in the book: not in `book.yml`, not in any earlier section (first use in the assembled
   booklet is here). Fix: at first use, "This book's pass condition, which the course calls its gate,
   asks ..."; or define it once in `how_to_read`. **gap**, **recurring** (term used before it is
   explained). (from compression hole 2, H17)

3. **must_know[2].** Claim: "That is Level 2B: a test you score, on the objective's action. Give it a
   week or more after the session". Source: Frich's Table 1 defines 2B by how it is measured, and its
   time frame is "learned during or by the end of the program, measured by objective means". An
   end-of-session scored test is Level 2B in Frich's terms. The week's delay is this book's own
   requirement, from section 1's definition of learning, not part of the level. Fix: "Any test you
   score against a key is Level 2B, including one at the end of the session. This book's gate asks
   for more: give it a week or more after the session, because section 1 counts only what lasts."
   Same in retrieval_items[2]. **error** (from compression hole 3, H18)

4. **must_know[3] and exercises[3].answer.** Claim: "Reaction and learning can point opposite ways"
   (refs Deslauriers); "People can rate one kind of teaching above another and learn less from it. A
   randomised study of university physics students found exactly that." Source: Deslauriers's test
   was taken "At the end of each class period". Section 1 of this book defines that as
   *performance*, not learning ("Performance is what a learner can do during a session or within
   minutes of it"). So this section states the finding further than it was measured, and against
   the book's own terms. Fix: must_know[3]: "Ratings and a test score at the end of class can point
   opposite ways." The answer: "... and score lower on a test at the end of the class. A randomised
   study of university physics students found exactly that." **error**, **recurring** (a finding
   stated beyond what was measured; SELFCHECK 4a)

5. **illustrations[1].body.** Claim: "Two observed any change in what physicians did at work." Source:
   "Two studies reported using objective outcome measures for behavior/expertise (Level 3B), such as
   using a form to score a third person's leadership performance ... or using a checklist to score a
   team's performance." These scored behaviour. The source does not say they observed a change.
   Fix: "Two measured behaviour at work objectively, for example with an observer's scoring form."
   **error**, **recurring** (stated beyond the source)

6. **illustrations[0].body, the Level 2B bullet.** Claim: "That is the test the section on measuring
   change scored." Section 12 scored a ten-question test for ten MBBS students, not a
   weight-plotting test for ASHA workers. Fix: "That is the kind of test, given at three times, that
   the section on measuring change scored." **error**, **recurring** (wrong pointer)

7. **illustrations[0].body, the table and the sentence after it.** Claim: "I will use this in my
   work. · an intention · 1", and "it belongs with reaction". Source: Frich's Level 1 is "How
   participants feel about the program and their satisfaction with different components". An
   intention to use is not in that description. Filing it at Level 1 is this book's own call. Fix:
   add "Frich's levels do not name intentions; this book files them with reaction because ..."
   **gap**

8. **illustrations[0].body.** "Anganwadi centre" is never explained in this book. "the block's usual

   centre)"; "the district block's usual form", or drop "block's". **gap**, **recurring** (term used
   before it is explained)

   (confirms the conductor's figure note)

10. **must_know[4].** It is tagged `kind: number` and carries no number. Fix: add one figure with its
