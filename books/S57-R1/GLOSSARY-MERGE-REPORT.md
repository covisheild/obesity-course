## 4. Drift inside S57-R1 (no record edited; each a possible fix pass item)

1. **feedback, three uses.** C01 uses "feedback form" (a learners' rating form) before C03 defines
   feedback as "showing the learner the correct answer after the attempt". C16 says role clarity "shows
   how feedback travels in the team", a third sense (word that work went wrong). The row records C03's
   sense only. Fix: C16 could say "how word of a problem travels".
2. **objective, noun and adjective.** C09 defines a learning objective; C13 defines "objective" (of how
   an outcome is measured). C13's Definition uses both in one paragraph ("A test that asks for the
   objective's action ... measures Level 2B"). Two rows with qualifiers keep them apart; C13 could add
   one line saying the adjective is not the objective of C09.
3. **gap before its definition.** C02 uses "gap" for the difference between two means (lines on the
   8-mark gap, the must-know on the SMD). C04 then defines the gap as the time between study sessions.
   Everyday use, minor; C02 could say "difference" instead.
4. **comparison group vs control group.** C02 names the comparison group; C05 quotes Dunlosky's
   "control group" and glosses it locally ("the group that only read"). Consistent, but the book never
   says the two names mean the same thing.
5. **evaluate.** C09 has "evaluate" as a Bloom level; C13 defines "to evaluate teaching" as measuring an
   outcome of it. Different senses; minor.
6. **leadership, two wordings.** C15: "to lead people is to get a group to move towards a change and to
   keep them at the work it takes". C16: Kotter's "leadership copes with change". Compatible; the row
   gives both with their records.
7. **retention interval.** C01: "the time between study and that test". C04: "the time from the last
   study session to the test". C04 sharpens, does not contradict; the row uses C01's words.
